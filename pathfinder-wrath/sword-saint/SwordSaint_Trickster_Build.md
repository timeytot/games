# Sword Saint 20 / Trickster / Bardiche

Human Sword Saint 20. Alignment Chaotic Neutral. Background Pickpocket. Chosen weapon Bardiche.

| Ability | Score |
|---|---:|
| Strength | 18 |
| Dexterity | 12 |
| Constitution | 14 |
| Intelligence | 16 |
| Wisdom | 8 |

Put every ability increase at levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 20 into Strength. Final Strength is 23.

Level 1 grants Weapon Focus (Bardiche) only. Weapon Specialization is a feat you take later. Stay unarmored and do not use a shield. Armor disables Canny Defense.

This plan follows the Sword Saint section of the protagonist guide: two-handed Bardiche, the Cleave line, crit attacks of opportunity, and Trickster Perception 2. The guide does not list skills. The skill row below is the practical allocation for the five points on the creation screen.

## Skills

At level 11 the spent ranks are Athletics 10, Knowledge (Arcana) 10, Knowledge (World) 10, Persuasion 10, Perception 5, Use Magic Device 5. Stop adding Perception and Persuasion.

Each later level has 5 points. Spend them in this order until that skill's ranks equal the current level:

1. Athletics
2. Knowledge (Arcana)
3. Knowledge (World)
4. Use Magic Device

Perception 2 is a Mythic Trick. Ranks in the Perception skill do not unlock Improved Improved Critical.

Athletics 3 raises Base Attack Bonus to the character's Athletics ranks. A level 20 Sword Saint otherwise has BAB +15. With Athletics at 20 ranks, BAB becomes 20. Until then, use Transformation to fill BAB.

## Combat

Use the Cleave line on groups, with the Enduring Blade enchantment left on the weapon. Against a high-AC target, spend the swift action on one arcana. Open with Prescient Attack. On the next round, use Dimension Strike as the swift action, then full attack. Use Arcane Accuracy on rounds when the other two are not active. Perfect Strike maximizes the weapon damage dice. On a confirmed critical hit, spend the extra Arcane Pool point to raise the critical multiplier by 1.

Party support from the guide: one domain caster (Luck, Madness, Law, Community, or Glory) and one Skald. The Skald supplies stats, attack, AC, extra critical multiplier, and Pounce. The late main-campaign weapon in that guide is its named endgame Bardiche.
