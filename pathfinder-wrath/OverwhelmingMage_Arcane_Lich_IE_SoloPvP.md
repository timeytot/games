# Overwhelming Mage 20 / Arcane Bloodline / Lich MR10

## Solo 1v1 role

This is the Charisma-based spontaneous Lich alternative: **Human Overwhelming Mage 20, Arcane Bloodline, merged Lich spellbook**. Take Overwhelming Mage at every class level and choose Lich at MR3.

The rules allow normal self-buffs, the Lich's Skeletal Champion, legal self-summons, and owned finite consumables. No external party members, imported enemy armies, unlimited summon loops, reset loops, or duplicated unique equipment are assumed. Buff and summon timers run normally.

Overwhelming Mage offers additional SR penetration and a limited DC setup ability. It does not acquire Divination's fixed-20 initiative feature. The [ordinary Divination Wizard / Lich](./WOTR_Lich_Complete_Endgame_Build_EN.md) remains the default Lich recommendation against unknown optimized opponents. This alternative has no measured PvP win-rate or numerical ranking.

Put the Human +2 and all five level-up increases into CHA. At level 20, CHA is 25 before equipment and permanent rewards. The pre-racial point costs are -4 + 5 + 5 + 2 + 0 + 17 = 25.

INT is useful for skills but is not this Sorcerer's casting attribute. After the complete Lich transformation, CHA also contributes to undead HP; CON is no longer the ordinary living-character HP attribute.

Prioritize Use Magic Device, Knowledge (Arcana), and Perception. Spend remaining ranks on Knowledge (World) and Persuasion.

## Metamagic and Overwhelming Spell

Overwhelming Penetration reaches +4 on checks against SR at class level 19. That bonus is not extra real CL for spell damage, duration, or dispel resistance.

At level 6, Overwhelming Spell can sacrifice a spell slot of level 3 or higher to improve the DC of the next spell cast within its stated two-round window. A level-9 sacrifice supplies +3 DC. Its swift activation competes with a rod-quickened spell for the same swift action.

- **Heighten:** raise the slot level and DC of a compatible lower-level save spell.
- **Persistent:** use on compatible save-based control such as Embrace of Death or Icy Prison, Mass, within available slots. It supplies no benefit to a no-save Corrupt Magic.
- **Favorite Metamagic: Persistent:** reduces the normal +2 slot adjustment to +1. Do not assume a slot above level 10 exists.
- **Quicken feat:** the normal +4 slot increase still applies. A level-9 Corrupt Magic cannot fit in a level-13 slot.
- **Greater Quicken Rod:** can quicken eligible spells through level 9, including Corrupt Magic; it cannot quicken level-10 Absolute Death.
- **Bolster:** use only with damage spells whose actual metamagic interface permits it.
- **Extend:** use on eligible timed defenses and summons, while tracking real elapsed time.

Absolute Death is already level 10. Do not Heighten, Persistent, or Quicken it into a nonexistent higher-level slot.

Ordinary spontaneous metamagic may require a full-round cast. Account for that before moving or planning a second action. Use Metamagic Adept's actual limited resource, Quicken, or a valid item effect only where it applies.

## Buffs, summons, and duel routine

### Preparation

1. Cast the applicable long-duration defenses: Mage Armor, Shield, Mind Blank, True Seeing, Echolocation, Foresight, Protection from Spells, Bone Shield, False Grace, Seamantle, and Greater Invisibility or Displacement.
2. Bone Shield and Shield have overlapping shield bonuses; use the higher. Different temporary-HP effects do not automatically add together.
3. Summon the finite group late enough to retain useful duration. The normal Skeletal Champion and available Lich summons may attack, cast, block space, and take their own actions.
4. Apply Haste, Heroism, and other compatible group buffs after the recipients exist. Higher morale buffs not in this known-spell list require an actual finite consumable source.
5. Cast the shortest defenses last and begin without pausing timers. External teammates and pre-collected enemy armies are excluded.

### Opening

Use a rod-quickened Corrupt Magic plus the remaining standard action when that combination is legal. For a higher-DC control opening, spend the swift action on Overwhelming Spell instead; do not also spend that swift action on a quickened dispel. A spontaneous metamagic cast may consume the full-round action.

Choose the follow-up from the enemy's remaining defenses: control, Clashing Rocks or an appropriate Lich damage spell, another dispel, or a summon. Use Deadly Magic during the important casting window. Keep distance from a Sword Saint's melee range and give the skeleton its own orders.

A target's Trick Fate does not replace the Lich's dispel dice, but it does protect the target's relevant saving throws. Corrupt Magic has no target save or SR check and still needs dispel checks. Absolute Death is not an automatic kill through successful saves, immunity, or Last Stand.

Against an enemy Lich, retain critical immunity and examine dispel resistance, undead defenses, and the ability to keep casting. Against a summon army, consider area control or removal rather than automatically attacking each unit.

This build cannot self-cast Trick Fate. It also cannot be credited with infinite scrolls, permanent Deadly Magic, unrestricted swift actions, or the Divination Wizard's initiative capstone.
