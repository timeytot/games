# Divination Wizard 20 / Lich MR10 — Natural Solo 1v1 Build

## Recommendation and rules

Use **ordinary Wizard 20, Divination school, merged Lich spellbook** as the default prepared solo-duel Lich. This is a mechanics-based recommendation against an unknown optimized opponent, not a demonstrated universal winner.

The comparison includes all DLC, level 20 / Mythic Rank 10, turn-based combat, an approximately 10-metre starting separation, and equal difficulty rules for both sides. Use a fixed legal character and owned equipment inventory. Normal class/path companions and finite self-summons may participate; external party members, imported armies of captured enemies, unlimited preparation loops, duplicated unique equipment, and combat resets are excluded. Buff and summon durations continue to run normally.

Follow one natural level-1-to-20 progression. No Toybox feat grants, Hilor retraining, final respec, prerequisite bypass, class dip, or inherited features from a discarded mythic path are required. This file supersedes the previous reservoir-based Wizard plan.

A direct-entry Inevitable Excess character can follow the same class and feat order, but does not automatically possess main-campaign rewards. An item's availability must come from that character's actual legal inventory. Owning a DLC alone does not place every reward in an IE character's inventory.

## Why keep all twenty Wizard levels?

Forewarned adds half the Wizard level to initiative and, at Wizard 20, fixes the initiative die at 20. A class dip or Wizard 10 / Loremaster 10 does not obtain this Wizard-20 feature merely because the merged spellbook has CL30.

Before Dexterity, equipment, or other bonuses, the finished opening contains:

| Source | Initiative contribution |
|---|---:|
| Forewarned die | Fixed 20 |
| Forewarned school bonus | +10 |
| Hare familiar | +4 |
| Pickpocket | +2 |
| Improved Initiative | +4 |
| Improved Initiative (Mythic), MR10 | +10 |
| Total before DEX / equipment | **50** |

Do not add INT directly to this Wizard's initiative. That is a Sword Saint feature. Fixed 20 does not guarantee first place against another fixed-20 character; compare the full totals.

Foretell is an additional Divination tool. Its supportive branch grants a luck bonus to relevant rolls, including caster-level checks in the ability description; it is not extra real caster level for buff duration or dispel resistance. Pay its normal activation action and round resource. Confirm its contribution in the combat-log breakdown, and do not add a second overlapping luck bonus.

Summoning feats are not included in this fixed plan. Normal summons and the Skeletal Champion already provide bodies and actions; the retained feat budget supports the main caster's dispelling and control. Do not assume Augment Summoning enhances every undead-creation or path-companion ability.

The skeleton selections are path progression, not extra generic mythic feats. Its Cleric/Bard upgrades do not turn it into a separate level-20 merged caster. Give the Marksman a legally obtained +8 mental-stat headband: its base WIS is only 8, so the Cleric upgrade alone does not unlock useful cleric casting. Equip a legally obtained Phylactery if available. It provides Deadly Magic access or additional uses according to its tooltip; this plan already selects Deadly Magic and does not depend on receiving the item at character creation.

### Metamagic boundaries

- Extend: use for compatible timed defenses and summons; timers still run.
- Persistent: use on compatible save-based control. Corrupt Magic has no saving throw, so Persistent adds no value to it.
- Favorite Metamagic: Persistent reduces the normal slot increase; it does not create an 11th- or 12th-level slot.
- Absolute Death is already level 10. Do not write an ordinary heightened/persistent/quickened version into a nonexistent higher slot.
- Bolster: use only on compatible damage spells shown in the preparation interface.
- Selective: apply only to eligible spells. Do not assume every ongoing hazard spares your summons.

## Summons and companions in this ruleset

Allowed resources include the Lich's normal Skeletal Champion and legal self-cast summons such as Animate Dead, Create Undead, Summon Monster, and path summons available in the character's actual book. They may attack, cast their own spells, occupy space, and receive legal allied buffs.

The caster and every unit still need their own actions. The principal uses are screening approaches, creating targeting pressure, supplying secondary attacks, and forcing the opponent to spend area damage or control. Do not assume a summon can automatically prevent charge, teleportation, or a path-specific movement ability.

1. Prepare the skeleton using its normal upgrade choices.
2. Cast long-duration personal defenses.
3. Create the finite summon group late enough that its real duration covers the fight.
4. Apply compatible group buffs after the recipients exist.
5. Cast the shortest buffs last and begin the duel without pausing their timers.

Lord Beyond the Grave benefits eligible controlled undead, not every living summoned creature. Offensive negative-energy effects and allied living summons also need their normal targeting checks; do not assume all of your area damage is harmless to your own army.

Enemy creatures collected through Repurpose outside the arena are not part of this self-summoning benchmark. A captured-enemy-army format would require a separate roster limit. No free enemy corpses are assumed for Power from Death.

## Duel opening

### Before initiative

Self-cast Mage Armor, Shield, Bone Shield, Mind Blank, Foresight, Seamantle, and the available protection package. Add sensory and concealment defenses, the finite summon group, and Haste in an order that preserves their durations.

Choose a compatible final polymorph layer. Fiery Body and Frightful Aspect are not two independent polymorph bonuses to add together. Fiery Body provides critical immunity; Bone Shield is a separate critical-immunity source. Bone Shield and Shield also supply overlapping shield bonuses: apply the higher, not their sum. Multiple temporary-HP effects do not automatically add together.

### First effective turn

1. Check the actual initiative order, range, hostile spell protections, and the enemy's visible defenses.
2. Activate the limited Deadly Magic window when the intended spell benefits from it.
3. Remove key enemy effects with **Corrupt Magic**. Use the Greater Quicken Rod if a swift dispel is needed and legal.
4. Use the remaining standard action for the effect the matchup requires: additional dispelling, compatible control, damage, or another summon.
5. Use Absolute Death when its Will-save branch is appropriate; a successful save still takes damage. It is not an assumed kill through Trick Fate or Last Stand.
6. Give the skeleton and summons their own orders and actions.

Without a separate action-reduction effect, a standard Corrupt Magic and a standard Absolute Death do not fit into the same turn. With a valid mask effect, allocate its free-action spell, one swift action, and one standard action explicitly. Do not count the mask twice or assume the rod can quicken Absolute Death.

## Checks that matter

Merged book baseline: Wizard 20 + Lich 10 = CL30. Dark Rites, equipped item bonuses, School Mastery, and spell specialization must be recorded separately.

For a dispel, distinguish actual caster level from dispel-only bonuses:

`d20 + relevant caster level + Dispel Focus bonuses + applicable check bonuses`

Compare with the affected spell's actual dispel DC, normally `11 + that buff's caster level`. School Mastery: Abjuration and specialization in Corrupt Magic concern the appropriate spell; Necromancy DC feats do not increase a Corrupt Magic check. Goggles can fix a limited dispel die at 20, not guarantee success against every possible DC.

Against a crit-based attacker, retain critical immunity. Against another caster, preserve the ability to act and cast after enemy dispels. Against a summon army, compare area removal/control with attacking the summoner. Against an opponent under Trick Fate, do not rely on a failed saving throw until that defense is removed or expires.

No claim here assumes that Deadly Magic bypasses every immunity, that a dispel removes class features such as Last Stand, or that all damage can finish an opponent during a death-prevention window.

## Alternative Lich chassis: result of the audit

Shadowcaster 20 with Divination is a genuine competitor: it can preserve Forewarned and adds its own shadow abilities. It trades away Arcane Bond and four bonus feats, and its +5 profane INT does not stack with a higher profane INT reward. Do not dismiss it using old reports about its shadow rebelling; do not assume it is automatically stronger either.

Scroll Savant 20 with Divination can preserve the school capstone and improve finite scroll use. Its value depends on the specified owned scroll inventory. Neither alternative has been shown to replace this full ordinary-Diviner configuration across the stated matchups.

Allowing summons strengthens the Lich army, but also restores Aivu for Azata, Aeon summon interactions where implemented, and normal Trickster summons. It does not prove an overall winner. Swarm must specify Swarm Size, finite clone count, and real equipment allocation before being compared.
