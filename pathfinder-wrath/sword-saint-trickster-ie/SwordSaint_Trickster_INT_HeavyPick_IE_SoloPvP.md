# Sword Saint 20 / Trickster MR10 / Death's Consonant Heavy Pick

## Scope and verdict

This is the all-DLC natural level-1-to-20 version, including Lord of Nothing. Choose Sword Saint at level 1, choose Trickster at Mythic Rank 3, and stay Trickster. Do not start as Trickster and later become Legend. The build assumes the local 2.7.0x rules, no party buffs, self-preparation, finite scrolls and rods, turn-based combat, Last Stand, Fortification, concealment, spell resistance and immunities all working.

The build is rated for unknown optimized solo opponents rather than a PvE boss. Its default weapon is the **Death's Consonant Heavy Pick**. The weapon is only +3, but Greater Magic Weapon at caster level 20 or Arcane Weapon can bring its total enhancement to +5; the Intelligence-for-attack-and-damage property remains. The +3 is therefore not an endgame problem.

**Final recommendation:** Human Sword Saint 20, Intelligence level-ups, empty off-hand, Death's Consonant Heavy Pick, Trickster Perception II, Knowledge (World) III, Lore (Religion) II and Use Magic Device III. Take Lore (Religion) I before Lore (Religion) II and use Nethys so Magic and Protection are available as the two domain choices. This document contains one natural progression only.

**Solo-PvP score: 9.4/10.** Damage 9.5, opening reliability 9.5, anti-caster 9.0, defense 8.8, resource independence 8.7. The score assumes finite scrolls and no imported Trickster-to-Legend critical chain.

## Natural ordinary feat order

This is the only feat order in this document. It is the legal level-by-level route for a new character; it does not use World III to bypass early prerequisites and does not require a later Hilor respec.

| Character level | Feat |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **Power Attack**; **Improved Initiative** (Human bonus) |
| 3 | **Focused Strike** (Lord of Nothing; requires STR 13 and Power Attack) |
| 5 | **Combat Reflexes**; Sword Saint bonus: **Blind Fight** |
| 7 | **Weapon Specialization: Heavy Pick**; Fighter Training is now active |
| 9 | **Critical Focus**; Sword Saint Critical Perfection now lets Magus level 9 satisfy the BAB prerequisite |
| 11 | **Improved Critical: Heavy Pick**; Sword Saint bonus: **Greater Weapon Focus: Heavy Pick** |
| 13 | **Staggering Critical** |
| 15 | **Improved Improved Critical: Heavy Pick** after Perception II is available |
| 17 | **Improved Improved Improved Critical: Heavy Pick**; Sword Saint bonus: **Stunning Critical** |
| 19 | **Critical Mastery** |

At level 3, Critical Focus is unavailable because the character has BAB 2. Focused Strike removes the Power Attack attack penalty from the first attack each turn when the one-handed Heavy Pick is held in two hands; the empty off-hand makes that mode available. Sword Saint's Critical Perfection arrives at level 9 and allows Magus level to replace BAB for feat prerequisites. Fighter Training arrives at level 7 and counts as Fighter level `Sword Saint level - 3`, making Weapon Specialization legal at that point.

This natural route prioritizes the playable critical/control package and Greater Weapon Focus. At level 11, the Sword Saint bonus slot cannot see Improved Improved Critical until Perception II is active, so Greater Weapon Focus is the correct legal choice there. Perception II must be taken at the first available rank-2 Trick slot; the Trickster critical feats then enter the normal slots at levels 15 and 17. This route does not use World III to retroactively add feats or create extra feat slots.

If Archmage Armor is unavailable at MR7, take Ever Ready instead. Archmage Armor requires a self-cast Mage Armor effect; a scroll/potion Mage Armor does not activate it. Once active, it is the armor layer, so do not count ordinary Bracers of Armor on top of it.

The important Trickster route is:

- Perception I → Perception II: unlocks the expanded critical chain.
- Knowledge (World) I → II → III: the second rank changes natural 1s to 20s; rank III is taken naturally at MR7 and is not used to bypass earlier feat prerequisites.
- Infuse Magic Device → Reuse Magic Device → Use Magic Device III: grants a Wizard spellbook that can copy scrolls as a Wizard of character level.
- Lore (Religion) I → II: rank II grants two domains and one daily use of spells below your mythic rank. With Nethys, use Magic and Protection for extra dispel and defensive options. This is the fresh-start default when the opponent pool includes Mind Blank, fear immunity or paralysis immunity.
- Trickery I remains useful as a dispel check, but Trickery II is a situational anti-buff variant rather than the default: it is a standard action, has no ranged use, and applies a -20 penalty to the Trickery check.

Do not use the Trickster feats as a bridge into Legend. This document is the direct Trickster version only.

### Weapon enhancement

Use **Greater Magic Weapon** from the UMD Wizard spellbook at caster level 20, or spend Arcane Pool points on the Sword Saint weapon enchantment, until the displayed total enhancement is +5. The +3 weapon and a +5 spell do not become +8; the tooltip uses the applicable highest enhancement instead. The Death's Consonant Intelligence effect is retained.

## Wizard spellbook and daily preparation

Use UMD III to copy scrolls into the Wizard spellbook. The Sword Saint book remains useful for Magus combat spells; the Wizard book supplies the high-level defensive and anti-caster package.

### Long-duration preparation after resting

- Mage Armor; Archmage Armor is active.
- Greater Magic Weapon on Death's Consonant.
- Mind Blank.
- Foresight.
- True Seeing.
- Freedom of Movement (external divine scroll/wand/consumable; it is not a Wizard-book spell).
- Death Ward (external divine scroll/wand/consumable; it is not a Wizard-book spell).
- Protection from Spells.
- Mirror Image, where the current duration qualifies for Enduring/Greater Enduring.
- Stoneskin.
- Heroic Invocation or Greater Heroism.
- Echolocation if the opponent can use invisibility or concealment.

Use Extend where needed so the duration qualifies for Greater Enduring Spells. Do not rely on an ordinary +AC number: Touch AC, miss chance, saves, immunity layers and initiative matter more.

### Short preparation immediately before entering combat

1. Shield if it is not already enduring.
2. Mirror Image if it is not enduring.
3. Greater Invisibility or Displacement; use the one that matches the opponent's True Seeing and concealment package.
4. Haste.
5. Vampiric Shadow Shield or another current defensive layer.
6. **Trick Fate** immediately before contact. It lasts three rounds and makes the target roll 20s.

Do **not** put Transformation in this default sequence. It forbids normal spellcasting and magic-item activation for its duration. Dimension Strike, Prescient Attack and Arcane Accuracy are Magus abilities rather than spells and remain usable, but Dispel, Wizard-book spells and wand/scroll actions do not. If you use this optional melee-only mode, cast Transformation and Trick Fate before contact; casting either after initiative costs the round's normal action and removes the clean first-round full attack.

Do not cast Trick Fate after the fight has started unless you have the IE Greater Quicken Rod ready. Trick Fate is a 7th-level spell and the rod makes it a Swift action; the same round can still full-attack if you are already in melee, but the Swift slot cannot also be used for Dimension Strike or Prescient Attack. Without the rod, an ordinary cast consumes the Standard action and loses that round's normal attack. If short-duration pre-casting is disallowed and no rod charge is available, use the lower non-Trick-Fate profile.

## Opening sequence and action economy

The default opening is:

1. Toggle Perfect Strike and the critical toggle.
2. Pre-apply Arcane Pool enhancement or confirm GMW +5.
3. Enter combat with Trick Fate active.
4. During the Trick Fate window, leave the swift action empty unless Prescient Attack's flat-footed/Canny-defense interaction is specifically useful. Dimension Strike is usually redundant because Trick Fate already replaces attack and confirmation rolls with 20.
5. Use a normal full attack with the Heavy Pick; the empty off-hand preserves Spell Combat for the separate case where you intentionally embed a Magus spell. After Trick Fate expires, use **Dimension Strike (Swift) → full attack** against high AC. Do not call a plain full attack Spell Combat.
6. If the opponent survives Last Stand, switch to normal full attacks, control, Greater Dispel or Wizard-book options rather than repeating a blind critical gamble.

Dimension Strike, Prescient Attack and Arcane Accuracy compete for the swift-action economy. True Strike is a Standard-action spell when cast alone; it can instead be embedded in Spell Combat as a full-round action, giving only the first attack its bonus and applying Spell Combat's normal attack penalty. Dimension Strike is the default accuracy button outside the Trick Fate window because it targets Touch AC; during Trick Fate, it is usually unnecessary.

## Matchup rules

- **Against ordinary martial builds:** Dimension Strike + Trick Fate is the primary kill window.
- **Against Fortification or Lich Bone Shield:** critical damage is not the plan. Use Wizard-book Greater Dispel or the Magic-domain dispel option; if you deliberately chose the situational Trickery II variant, use it only after confirming the check can reach the target's caster level. Bone Shield specifically grants critical-hit immunity. Corrupt Magic is a Lich tool, not a Sword Saint spell.
- **Against Aeon:** expect buffs to be stripped. Keep Mind Blank, Freedom of Movement, Death Ward and a second defensive layer; do not spend every resource on a single opening attack.
- **Against Last Stand:** first-round damage is only a tempo advantage. Dual Kukri is a viable alternate chassis if the rule set rewards long fights.
- **Against a full merged Lich/Angel caster:** this build is no longer the strongest character in the rules. A complete merged caster has better caster level, spell DC and dispel pressure than a Sword Saint with a UMD book.
