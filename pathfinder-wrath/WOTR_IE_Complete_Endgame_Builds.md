# Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous — IE Complete Endgame Builds

This is the compact, actionable build file for **Inevitable Excess (IE)**. It keeps one strongest finished build per system, in score order. The scores are internal Unfair/min-max comparisons, not official ratings.

The Angel, Lich, and Sword Saint sections now give one natural level-1-to-20 class/feat progression each. They do not rely on a World III prerequisite bypass, Hilor retraining, or a final respec; IE direct-entry characters may still use the same choices in the level-up UI.

IE has two separate entry modes:

- **Mythic entry:** character level 20 with Mythic Rank 10. The five builds below are written for this mode.
- **Fresh Legend entry:** select Legend at MR3 and reach character level 40. A fresh Legend cannot later gain Angel, Lich or Trickster path features. The full ranked builds below therefore do not pretend that those paths are available in fresh Legend.

The guide does not use the main-campaign sequence where a character starts as Angel, Lich or Trickster and changes to Legend at MR8. For the fresh Legend fallback, use Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 / Mutation Warrior 20 or Inciter Skald 20 / Mutation Warrior 20; those are compatibility plans, not additional ranked variants.

## 1. Angel Oracle — 9.9/10 SS

### Final build

**Seeker Oracle 16 / Scaled Fist Monk 1 / Paladin 2 / Hellknight 1 / Angel MR10**

This is the strongest all-purpose IE main: it combines very high Charisma defenses with a merged Angel spellbook, reliable healing, communal buffs and high-damage Angel bolts.

### Creation

| Field | Choice |
|---|---|
| Race | Aasimar (Angelkin) |
| Alignment | Lawful Good; required for the Paladin/Hellknight shell |
| Background | Pickpocket |
| Mystery | Nature |
| Curse | Hellbound |
| Deity | Sarenrae |
| Starting stats | STR 16, DEX 7, CON 14–16, INT 10, WIS 11, CHA 21 |
| Level-ups | Put every normal increase into CHA (to 26) |
| Skills | Max Persuasion, Use Magic Device, Knowledge (Religion), Perception and Mobility 3 |

**Nature's Whispers** converts the important AC contribution to Charisma. Scaled Fist adds Charisma to AC, Paladin 2 supplies Divine Grace, and Hellknight 1 supplies Smite Chaos.

### Class order and key choices

| Character level | Class | Key choice |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Seeker Oracle 1 | Nature's Whispers; Improved Initiative |
| 2 | Scaled Fist Monk 1 | Dodge |
| 3–9 | Seeker Oracle 2–8 | Crane Style, Outflank, Power Attack and the Wolf companion revelation |
| 10 | Paladin 1 | Divine Grace |
| 11–18 | Seeker Oracle 9–16 | Spell focus/penetration feats and final revelations |
| 19 | Hellknight 1 | Smite Chaos; choose the lawful order that matches the character |
| 20 | Paladin 2 | Second Paladin level and final Oracle package |

Use this natural feat order; it does not assume a respec or illegal early prerequisites:

| Character level | Ordinary feat | Seeker bonus magic feat |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Improved Initiative | — |
| 2 | — | Monk bonus: Dodge |
| 3 | Crane Style | — |
| 5 | Power Attack | — |
| 6 | — | Spell Focus: Conjuration |
| 7 | Outflank | — |
| 9 | Boon Companion if the Nature Animal Companion revelation is active; otherwise Spell Penetration | — |
| 11 | Spell Penetration if it was not taken at level 9 | — |
| 12 | — | Greater Spell Penetration |
| 13 | Greater Spell Focus: Conjuration | — |
| 15 | Bolster Spell | — |
| 17 | Heighten Spell | Quicken Spell |
| 19 | Spell Specialization: Bolt of Justice, or a defensive feat | — |

Seeker bonus feats at Oracle 5/10/15 are restricted to the magic-feat list; they cannot provide weapon feats. The Battle Weapon Mastery revelation from the MR3 Second Mystery supplies Greataxe Weapon Focus, Improved Critical at Oracle 8, and Greater Weapon Focus at Oracle 12 without spending ordinary or Seeker feat slots. Spell Specialization at level 19 targets Bolt of Justice; replace it with Storm of Justice if that is the primary Angel spell in your current spellbook. Shatter Defenses is omitted because this natural route does not spend extra slots on Dazzling Display and a demoralize chain.

### Mythic and Angel progression

| Rank | Generic choice | Angel choice or priority |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Last Stand | Survive the first boss burst |
| 2 | Master Shapeshifter | Strength, Dexterity and transformation scaling |
| 3 | Second Mystery: Battle | Select Angel here and merge the Oracle/Angel spellbook |
| 4 | Mythic Improved Critical: Greataxe | Improve the 2H melee threat range |
| 5 | Abundant Smite | More reliable boss smites |
| 6 | Mythic Power Attack | Convert the high Strength into damage |
| 7 | Enduring Spells | Long-duration Angel and Oracle buffs |
| 8 | Mythic Weapon Focus: Greataxe | Accuracy for the melee shell |
| 9 | Greater Enduring Spells | Make the defensive package last through the day |
| 10 | Favorite Metamagic: Extend | Keep the full Angel/Oracle buff package running all day |

### Spell priorities

- **Long-duration buffs:** Shield of Faith, Resist Energy Communal, Death Ward, Freedom of Movement, True Seeing Communal, Greater Angelic Aspect, Mind Blank and Legendary Proportions.
- **Control and support:** Greater Dispel Magic, Prayer, Frightful Aspect, Heal, Greater Restoration and Mass Heal.
- **Angel offense:** Bolt of Justice for a single target, Storm of Justice for groups, then the strongest available Angel damage spell from the merged book. Use Sword of Heaven before entering the encounter.

### How to play

Before combat, let the BFT and Cleric apply the communal package, then cast Guarded Hearth when the encounter justifies it. Put Sword of Heaven and the long-duration Angel defenses up before initiative. On the opening turn, remove a boss defense with Greater Dispel if necessary, then cast Bolt/Storm of Justice. Use Quickened Heal or Greater Dispel only when it preserves the main Angel action. Keep healing and condition removal for emergencies; do not spend the first round on small heals while the primary threat is alive.

### Recommended party

1. Angel Oracle above.
2. Inciter Skald 20 for the martial multiplier.
3. Brown-Fur Transmuter 20 for shared size, Dexterity and weapon buffs.
4. Cleric 20 for Guarded Hearth and communal defenses.
5. Mutation Warrior 19 / Demonslayer Ranger 1 as the full-BAB front line.
6. Stigmatized Witch 20 for Evil Eye, Protective Luck and Cackle.

## 2. Lich caster — 9.6/10 Boss ranking; 9.4/10 Solo PvP ranking

> **Authoritative full configuration:** [WOTR_Lich_Complete_Endgame_Build_EN.md](WOTR_Lich_Complete_Endgame_Build_EN.md). This section keeps only the summary needed by the master table.
>
> The 9.6 and 9.4 scores are two independent scores for the same core: 9.6 is the prepared Unfair Boss score, while 9.4 is the prepared Solo PvP score against an unknown opponent without opponent-specific equipment swaps. The Boss version prioritizes reliability; the PvP version makes small substitutions to Lich Powers and spell preparation.

### Authoritative endgame build

**Dhampir（Vetala-Born/Ajibachana） / Exploiter Wizard 20 / Lich MR10**

This replaces the old `Stigmatized Witch 10 / Loremaster 9 / Crossblooded Sorcerer 1` Solo plan in this file. The old hybrid remains a party or legacy-version reference, but it is not the current vanilla, bug-free endgame Boss/PvP build. The pure Exploiter route keeps Wizard 20, full prepared casting, the CL30 merged spellbook, and all 10th-level Lich resources; it does not add Quick Study, Counterspell, or Metamixing, which are not present in the current vanilla Exploit list.

### Character-creation fields

| Field | Selection |
|---|---|
| Race | Dhampir — Vetala-Born/Ajibachana; Basic Elf is an alternative if you only want the highest SR checks |
| Alignment | NE or LE; NE recommended |
| Background | Pickpocket (+2 Initiative, Trickery/Stealth); Scholar–Arcane is the academic alternative |
| Deity | Urgathoa; Nethys is only a narrative alternative |
| Abilities | 25-point buy: STR 7 / DEX 14 / CON 14 / INT 17 / WIS 11 / CHA 14; after Vetala: DEX 16 / CON 14 / INT 19 / WIS 9 / CHA 14; put every level-up into INT, final INT 24 |
| Skills | Knowledge (Arcana/World/Religion), Perception, Spellcraft, Use Magic Device, Trickery; put remaining ranks into Mobility |

### Class, feats, and Exploits

- Take **Exploiter Wizard** at levels 1–20; choose Lich at MR3 and merge the spellbooks, reaching effective CL30 at endgame.
- Key feats: Spell Focus (Conjuration/Necromancy), Greater Spell Focus (Conjuration/Necromancy), Spell Penetration, Greater Spell Penetration, Selective Spell, Heighten Spell, Persistent Spell, Quicken Spell, Bolster Spell, Extend Spell, Blind Fight, and Improved Initiative; take Spell Specialization (Enervation/Boneshaker) as the level-20 Wizard bonus feat. Some versions may not list Lich-exclusive spells in this UI.
- Exploits: 1 Potent Magic; 5 Dimensional Slide; 9 Spell Resistance; 13 Greater Spell Resistance; 17 Arcane Barrier/Energy Shield. Potent Magic consumes limited Arcane Reservoir charges; it is not a permanent +2.

### Natural mythic and Lich choices

- Generic mythic order: Abundant Casting MR1; Mythic Spell Focus: Necromancy MR2; Enduring Spells MR3; Mythic Spell Penetration MR4; Greater Enduring Spells MR5; Mythic Improved Initiative MR6; Improved Abundant Casting MR7; Archmage Armor MR8; Greater Abundant Casting MR9; Last Stand or Mythic Resolve MR10.
- Lich path order: Danse Macabre at MR1; Negative Energy Insight at MR2; Indestructible Bones at MR3; Lord Beyond the Grave at MR4; Dark Rites at MR5; Withering Life at MR6; Skeletal Cleric I at MR8; Eclipse Chill at MR9; Skeletal Bard I at MR10.
- The Phylactery provides the Deadly Magic resource described in the full Lich file; it is not a separate respec or alternate level-up route.

### Spell and equipment summary

- Core spells: Corrupt Magic (multi-layer dispel and penalties with no SR/no save), Absolute Death (10th level, Will partial, no SR; Greater Quicken Rod cannot accelerate 10th-level spells), Negative Eruption, Feast of Blood, Siphon Life, Embrace of Death, Domain of Hungry Flesh, Siphon Time, Bone Shield, False Grace, Deny Death, Blessing of Unlife, Vampiric Blade, and Power from Death.
- Long-duration defenses: Mage Armor + Archmage Armor, Shield, Mind Blank, True Seeing, Echolocation, Foresight, Greater Invisibility/Displacement, Seamantle, Frightful Aspect, Freedom of Movement, Death Ward, energy protections, and Greater Heroism.
- Endgame equipment: Robe of Seven Sins (or one Necromancy-DC robe), Sin Mage's Staff/Quarterstaff of the War Mage, Heartstone of Night Hag Queen (SR38; use the current tooltip for natural AC because guides disagree between +5 and +7), Flawless Headband of Mental Perfection +8, Flawless Cloak of Resistance +7, Phylactery (wrist slot; +2 uses if Lich Deadly Magic is also selected), Gloves of Enduring Wizard, Belt of Physical Perfection, Boots of Perfect Grounding, Ring of Triumphant Advance + Magician's Ring/Caster's Dream; carry a Greater Quicken Rod, Grandmaster's Rod, and a Ghost Touch weapon or scroll as Nahyndrian Darkness backups.

### Boss/PvP trade-offs

- **Boss 9.6:** Start with Corrupt Magic, then use Negative Eruption/Feast of Blood/Absolute Death; use Last Stand, Bone Shield, temporary hit points, summons, and Dimensional Slide for a long fight. Against Nahyndrian Darkness, do not rely only on direct spells: handle the crystals and concealment/incorporeal defenses first, then use Ghost Touch/Weapon of Death, summons, or SR-free control as backups.
- **PvP 9.4:** Prepare Mind Blank, True Seeing, Greater Invisibility, Foresight, and Deny Death; use Corrupt Magic to dismantle defenses, Deadly Magic during key casting windows, and Absolute Death/control chains to close. Against high saves, death immunity, or counter-casters, do not treat one Absolute Death cast as the only win condition.

## 3. Kinetic Sharpshooter — 9.7/10 SS

### Final build

**Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 / Trickster MR10**, using a **Heavy Crossbow**.

Charged Ammunition uses the equipped ranged weapon's attack profile and critical profile, but normally delivers only one kinetic blast per round. Weapon Focus and Improved Critical must name the Heavy Crossbow; Kinetic Blast versions do not replace the weapon attack. Blue Flame targets Touch AC and checks Spell Resistance; Air is the physical-blast backup.

### Core progression

| Level | Feat or feature |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Point-Blank Shot; Human bonus Precise Shot; Fire; Charged Ammunition |
| 3 | Weapon Focus: Heavy Crossbow; Kinetic Quiver |
| 5 | Spell Penetration; Empowered Metakinesis |
| 7 | Deadly Aim; Fire again to unlock Blue Flame |
| 9 | Greater Spell Penetration; Maximized Metakinesis |
| 11 | Improved Critical: Heavy Crossbow; Rending Arrows |
| 13 | Improved Improved Critical: Heavy Crossbow after Perception II; Honed Infusion |
| 15 | Improved Improved Improved Critical: Heavy Crossbow; Expanded Element: Air |
| 17 | Improved Precise Shot; Pure-Flame |
| 19 | Improved Initiative; Chain Arrows; Metakinetic Master: Empowered |
| 20 | Celerity; Critical Overdrive; Infusion Specialization 6 |

Put normal level-up points into DEX. Max Perception, Stealth, Use Magic Device and Persuasion. Use Ascendant Element: Fire, Last Stand, Over-Infused Blasts only when the current patch accepts the two-substance interaction, and the Trickster Perception chain at MR3–4.

### Trickster sequence

Take Perception I at MR3, Perception II at MR4, then Knowledge (World) I/II/III and Persuasion I/II/III as the available Trickster slots permit. Trick Fate arrives at Trickster MR9. It is a three-round boss button; it does not remove concealment miss chance, Spell Resistance or Fortification.

### Combat routine

- No SR: **Blue Flame + Rending Arrows**.
- High SR: **Blue Flame + Pure-Flame**.
- Cluster: unaltered Blue Flame + Chain Arrows.
- Create Kinetic Quiver with 1 Burn and refresh it when charges run out.
- Use Empowered as the default single-blast metakinesis. Use Quicken only when the current combat log proves that it creates a second Charged Ammunition blast.
- Do not expect Rapid Shot, Manyshot or Vital Strike to create extra KSS blasts.

### Recommended party

1. KSS 20 / Trickster MR10.
2. Inciter Skald 20 for shared sneak dice, Lethal Stance and Beast Totem.
3. Brown-Fur Transmuter 20 for Greater Magic Weapon, Haste and shared transformations.
4. Cleric 20 for Guarded Hearth, True Seeing, Death Ward and Freedom of Movement.
5. Mutation Warrior 19 / Demonslayer Ranger 1 for a full-BAB flanking body.
6. Witch 20 or Oracle 20 for hexes, healing and condition removal.

## 4. Trickster Sword Saint — 9.7/10 SS

### Final build

**Sword Saint 20 / Trickster MR10**, using a two-handed **Bardiche**.

Dimension Strike converts the full attack into Touch attacks for the round. Trickster Perception II supplies the extra critical chain, while Arcane Pool, Prescient Attack and Arcane Accuracy solve the boss accuracy problem.

### Creation and class plan

| Field | Choice |
|---|---|
| Race | Human |
| Alignment | Chaotic Neutral |
| Background | Pickpocket |
| Stats | STR 18, DEX 12, CON 14, INT 16, WIS 8, CHA 7 |
| Level-ups | Put every normal increase into STR |
| Skills | Athletics, Knowledge (Arcana), Knowledge (World), Use Magic Device and Perception |
| Weapon | Bardiche; stay unarmored and use Canny Defense |

Take **Power Attack**, **Cleave**, **Cleaving Finish**, **Combat Reflexes**, **Great Cleave**, **Improved Critical: Bardiche**, **Outflank**, **Weapon Specialization: Bardiche**, then the Trickster Improved Critical chain and Improved Initiative. Do not waste the short feat budget on Shatter Defenses if the critical chain is incomplete.

### Magus arcana and mythic sequence

| Level/rank | Choice |
|---:|---|
| Magus 3 | Arcane Accuracy |
| Magus 6 | Enduring Blade |
| Magus 9 | Prescient Attack |
| Magus 12 | Dimension Strike |
| Magus 15 | Ghost Blade |
| Magus 18 | Devoted Blade |
| MR1 | Last Stand |
| MR2 | Mythic Power Attack |
| MR3 | Trickster; Knowledge (Arcana) I |
| MR4 | Mythic Improved Critical: Bardiche; Perception I and II |
| MR5 | Enduring Spells; Knowledge (World) I |
| MR6 | Mythic Power Attack; Knowledge (World) II |
| MR7 | Improved Initiative; Knowledge (World) III |
| MR8 | Greater Enduring Spells; Reuse Magic Device |
| MR9 | Ever Ready; Trick Fate becomes available |
| MR10 | Mythic Weapon Specialization: Bardiche; Athletics III |

Athletics III can raise the effective Base Attack Bonus toward the skill rank cap. Until it is online, Transformation supplies the missing martial accuracy for a hard encounter.

### Spell priorities

- **Defense:** Shield, Mirror Image, Blur, Displacement, Greater Invisibility, Stoneskin, Vampiric Shadow Shield and True Seeing.
- **Accuracy:** True Strike, Arcane Accuracy, Prescient Attack and Dimension Strike. These use the same swift-action economy, so choose one for the round.
- **Buffs:** Haste, Greater Magic Weapon, Enlarge Person Mass and Transformation.
- **Utility:** Greater Dispel Magic, Dimension Door and Frigid Touch.

### How to play

Before combat, cast Shield, Mirror Image, Displacement and the enduring buffs. Pre-apply the Arcane Weapon enhancement and Trick Fate when the rules allow short-duration pre-buffs. Keep Perfect Strike and the critical toggle on. During the Trick Fate window, use the full attack directly; its attack and confirmation rolls are already replaced by 20, so Dimension Strike is usually redundant. After Trick Fate expires, use **Dimension Strike (Swift) → full attack** against high AC. Do not try to activate Arcane Weapon or cast Trick Fate after Dimension Strike in the same round: the former competes for Swift and the latter is a Standard action. Prescient Attack is the alternative when flat-footed/Canny-defense interaction is more valuable. Do not enter melee without Mirror Image/Displacement active.

### Recommended party

1. Sword Saint 20 / Trickster MR10.
2. Inciter Skald 20 for flanking, shared sneak dice and melee damage.
3. Brown-Fur Transmuter 20 for GMW, Haste and shared transformations.
4. Cleric 20 for Guarded Hearth, Death Ward and healing.
5. Sable Company Marine 1 / Paladin 13 / Sohei 1 / Mutation Warrior 5 as the second front line.
6. Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 for ranged pressure.

## 5. Inciter Skald — 9.6/10 SS support

### Final build

**Inciter Skald 20 / Trickster MR10**.

This is the strongest retained Inciter because Trickster adds Perception critical support and Persuasion control while the Skald supplies the actual party engine.

### Core progression

| Level | Choice |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Lingering Performance; Spell Focus: Enchantment |
| 3 | Greater Spell Focus: Enchantment; Lethal Stance |
| 5 | Spell Penetration |
| 6 | Lesser Beast Totem |
| 7 | Greater Spell Penetration |
| 9 | Dispel Focus; Canny Observer |
| 11 | Extra Rage Power: Beast Totem |
| 12 | Greater Beast Totem; Sneaky Tricks |
| 13 | Greater Dispel Focus |
| 14 | Advanced Rogue Talent: Dispelling Attack |
| 15 | Come and Get Me |
| 17 | Deadly Accuracy |
| 18 | Lethal Accuracy |
| 19 | Selective Spell; Petrifying Strike |
| 20 | Master Skald; Hit a Nerve |

Use Kindred Half-Elf, high CHA, Pickpocket, and max Persuasion/Perception. The song shares sneak-attack dice, Lethal Stance and Beast Totem. Dispelling Attack is the boss tool; Petrifying Strike is the fallback when the current patch still has the old targeted-dispel bug.

### Mythic and Trickster sequence

Take Last Stand, Abundant Casting, Improved Abundant Casting, Mythic Inspiration, Enforced Vigor, Mythic Spell Penetration, Favorite Metamagic: Selective, Mythic Spell Focus: Enchantment, Inspirational Leader and Rupture Restraints. Use Perception I/II for the melee critical line, then Persuasion I/II/III for control, with Knowledge (World) and Infuse Magic Device in the remaining slots.

### Spells and combat loop

Prioritize Good Hope, Haste, Freedom of Movement, Greater Invisibility, Echolocation, Greater Heroism, Mass Cacophonous Call, Song of Discord, Mind Fog, Overwhelming Presence and Selective Confusion. Before combat, cast the long-duration package. In round one, activate **Raging Song** with Accept Rage only on the martial characters, then use Dispelling Attack or selective control. Let Sword Saint and Mutation Warrior full-attack while KSS stays at range. Turn the song off for caster rounds unless Hit a Nerve is confirmed to remove the patch's casting restriction.

### Recommended party

1. Inciter Skald 20 / Trickster MR10.
2. Sword Saint 20 / Trickster MR10.
3. Brown-Fur Transmuter 20.
4. Cleric 20.
5. Mutation Warrior 19 / Demonslayer Ranger 1.
6. Kinetic Sharpshooter 20.

## Fresh IE Legend fallback

Fresh Legend is selected at MR3 and cannot later become Angel, Lich or Trickster. If this is the mode being played, use only these two practical replacements:

| System | Fresh Legend replacement |
|---|---|
| Ranged finisher | Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 / Mutation Warrior 20 |
| Party engine | Inciter Skald 20 / Mutation Warrior 20 |

Do not label either replacement as an Angel, Lich or Trickster build. They retain only ordinary class levels and the two generic Mythic Hero choices available before the MR3 Legend selection.
