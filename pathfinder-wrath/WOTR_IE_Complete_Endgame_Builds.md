# Boss PvE Builds — Inevitable Excess

The earlier Boss-oriented builds are retained here as PvE references. They are separate from the solo 1v1 builds and may depend on allied support. The selections below have been corrected against installed game blueprints; this is not a PvP ranking or a completed boss-combat benchmark.

| Build | Finished class and path | PvE role |
|---|---|---|
| Angel Oracle | Seeker Oracle 16 / Scaled Fist 1 / Paladin 2 / Hellknight 1 / Angel | Divine casting, defenses and boss damage |
| Kinetic Sharpshooter | Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 / Trickster | Ranged boss damage |
| Inciter Skald | Inciter Skald 20 / Trickster | Martial support and enemy debuffs |

The Kinetic Sharpshooter and Inciter archetypes, and the Dispel Focus feats used below, require their A Dance of Masks content to be available. The route assumes those character-creation options are unlocked.

## Angel Oracle

### Final build

**Seeker Oracle 16 / Scaled Fist Monk 1 / Paladin 2 / Hellknight 1 / Angel MR10**

A retained party-supported PvE hybrid: merged Angel casting is its main boss tool, while the martial dips add saves, an unarmored AC option and alignment-dependent weapon smites. This is a legal reference route, not a claim that four noncasting levels outperform a full Oracle. Its base merged caster level is 26 at Oracle 16 / Angel 10; do not budget spells or durations as if it were Oracle 20 / Angel 10.

The installed Scaled Fist AC buff explicitly suppresses Nature's Whispers, so this route does **not** select or count Nature's Whispers as a second Charisma-to-AC contribution. Nature instead supplies a Wolf companion and Friend to Animals. Paladin **2**, not Paladin 1, supplies Divine Grace. Hellknight 1 supplies Smite Chaos; do not add its Charisma AC contribution to another smite without checking the active target's combat breakdown.

The Greataxe line supports occasional weapon attacks; Angel bolts remain the main boss offense.

**Duration limit:** Enduring Spells and Greater Enduring Spells affect qualifying spell buffs, not every feature or item ability. Extend must be applied to an eligible spell. A round/level buff is not automatically an all-day buff: at base merged CL26 an extended round/level duration is 52 rounds / 312 seconds, which can meet the five-minute threshold, but only where that spell's actual duration and Extend eligibility permit it. Sword of Heaven, smites and other limited features retain their own resource/duration rules. Favorite Metamagic does not by itself make every buff last 24 hours.

### Spell priorities

- **Oracle/Angel defenses and support:** Shield of Faith, Resist Energy Communal, Death Ward, Freedom of Movement, True Seeing Communal, Greater Angelic Aspect, Frightful Aspect, Heal, Greater Restoration and Mass Heal, as the merged book unlocks them.
- **Dispel and offense:** Greater Dispel Magic; Bolt of Justice for a single target and Storm of Justice for groups. Check the spell's actual school, save and resistance entry when applying focus or penetration bonuses; neither feat line is a universal Angel damage multiplier.
- **Allied support:** a Brown-Fur Transmuter can supply applicable arcane/transmutation buffs such as Legendary Proportions or Mind Blank. A domain Cleric supplies Guarded Hearth. These are party contributions, not spells this Oracle route promises to cast itself.

### How to play

Let the party apply its communal buffs and place Guarded Hearth when available. Apply eligible long-duration Oracle/Angel buffs and use Sword of Heaven before a planned encounter if its remaining duration covers the fight. Open with Greater Dispel when a removable boss defense matters, otherwise use Bolt/Storm of Justice. Use quickened healing or dispelling only when the active quickening source and the spell's displayed slot permit it; ordinary Quicken adds four spell levels, so a level-6 Heal cannot simply be budgeted in a level-9 slot. Use the Wolf and allied front line to occupy enemies. Smite Evil/Chaos and the Greataxe are conditional weapon tools; choose the appropriate target alignment and inspect the combat log before counting their effects.

## Kinetic Sharpshooter

### Final build and creation

**Human Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 / Trickster MR10**, using a **Heavy Crossbow**.

A ranged PvE boss-damage reference that expects allied buffs and a front line. Use post-racial **STR 9, DEX 19, CON 18, INT 10, WIS 10, CHA 7**. Pre-racial 9/17/18/10/10/7 costs -1+13+17+0+0-4 = 25; put Human's +2 in DEX. All five level increases go into DEX, reaching 24 before equipment. Choose Pickpocket. Prioritize Perception and Stealth; allocate other skills within the class's available ranks rather than promising four maxed skills with INT 10.

Charged Ammunition uses the equipped ranged weapon's attack and critical profile. Select **Heavy Crossbow** for the weapon feat chain; Kinetic Blast versions do not replace those weapon selections. Budget one Charged Ammunition blast per round until an actual combat log demonstrates an additional blast. Blue Flame is the energy composite option that targets Touch AC and checks Spell Resistance; Air supplies a physical option against ordinary AC without that energy-blast SR check.

### Combat routine

- Against a suitable single target: **Blue Flame + Rending Arrows**.
- When SR is the obstacle: **Blue Flame + Pure-Flame**.
- Against a cluster: **Blue Flame + Chain Arrows**, without adding a second substance infusion.
- Use the physical Air blast when its ordinary-AC attack is the better option; Ascendant Element (Fire) does not itself remove Blue Flame's SR check.
- Create Kinetic Quiver with its burn cost and refresh it when charges run out. Check the active quiver and burn before the encounter.
- Use Empowered as the default blast metakinesis. Count a Quickened extra Charged Ammunition blast only after a combat log confirms it on the installed game.
- Rapid Shot, Manyshot and Vital Strike are not included as sources of extra Charged Ammunition blasts. The selected crossbow feats improve the weapon attack that actually carries the blast.

## Inciter Skald

### Final build

**Inciter Skald 20 / Trickster MR10**.

A party support and debuff reference. Its value depends on allied martial attackers accepting Raging Song, their ability to hit and sneak attack, and the target's defenses. Trickster adds critical-feat access and conditional Persuasion control; this document does not rank it above every alternative Skald path.

### Creation and natural progression

Use Kindred-Raised Half-Elf with its flexible racial bonus also in CHA: **STR 12, DEX 14, CON 12, INT 10, WIS 9, CHA 22** after racial bonuses. The pre-racial 12/14/12/10/9/18 costs 2+5+2+0-1+17 = 25. Put all five increases into CHA, reaching 27 before equipment. Choose Pickpocket, and prioritize Persuasion and Perception within the available skill ranks.

The level-1 Spell Focus below uses the retained **Skald bonus magic feat**, not a second ordinary feat. Inciter replaces the normal rage-power slots at 9 and 15; every extra power below explicitly spends an ordinary feat.

| 2 | - | Inciter sneak-attack progression begins |

| 4 | - | Rogue talent: Canny Observer |

Raging Song shares the Inciter's relevant sneak-attack dice and selected rage powers with accepting allies. **Dispelling Attack** triggers on a damaging sneak attack and attempts a targeted dispel; it is not an independently cast action and does not guarantee stripping every defense. **Petrifying Strike** adds 2 Dexterity damage to eligible sneak attacks; despite its name, it does not directly petrify the target, and ability-damage immunity can prevent its effect.

### Spells and combat loop

Prioritize Good Hope, Haste, Freedom of Movement, Greater Invisibility, Echolocation, Greater Heroism, Mass Cacophonous Call, Song of Discord, Mind Fog, Overwhelming Presence and Selective Confusion as their spell levels unlock. Prepare the long-duration party package before combat, then activate Raging Song and use control or an attack that can actually deal sneak-attack damage. Allied Sword Saints and Mutation Warriors benefit from the martial package; KSS remains a ranged attacker.

Before level 20, manage **Accept Rage** so the song's ability-use restrictions do not obstruct allied casters. At level 20, **Hit a Nerve** explicitly removes Inspired Rage's AC penalty and ability-use restrictions for allies who accept it; Master Skald is replaced rather than also retained. This capstone correction is supported by blueprint data, not a claim that a particular modded save has been runtime-tested.
