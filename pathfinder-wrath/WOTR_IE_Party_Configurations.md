# IE Party Configurations for the Five Complete End States

This file gives compact six-person party shells for the five complete IE Mythic-entry systems in the main build guide. All five main-character lines below are **level 20 / Mythic Rank 10** builds. A fresh IE Legend is a different mode: it starts at MR3 and reaches character level 40, so it cannot use the Angel, Lich or Trickster path features listed here.

The class levels are the important part of each companion or mercenary chassis. A non-commander mercenary can use Mythic Companion choices; do not read every support row as a second required mythic path.

In each roster, **slot 1 is the commander/main character**. Slots 2–6 are mercenary or respec-companion blueprints; they provide the listed class abilities without requiring a second Angel, Lich or Trickster path.

## Reusable support chassis

| Chassis | Class levels | What it provides |
|---|---|---|
| Inciter support | Inciter Skald 20 | Inspired Rage/Raging Song, Lethal Stance, Beast Totem, shared sneak dice, enemy AC/Will pressure and targeted dispels |
| Transmutation support | Brown-Fur Transmuter 20 | Share Transmutation, Greater Magic Weapon, Legendary Proportions or Transformation, Haste, Echolocation and emergency concealment |
| Divine support | Cleric 20 (Sosiel chassis) | Guarded Hearth, communal defenses, Death Ward, Freedom of Movement, Heal, Greater Dispel and condition removal |
| Martial front line | Mutation Warrior 20 | Mutagen, weapon training, bonus feats, Outflank, Seize the Moment and reliable full attacks |
| Mounted front line | Sable Company Marine 1 / Paladin 13 / Sohei 1 / Mutation Warrior 5 | Hippogriff mobility, mounted full attacks, smites, auras and a second durable front line |
| Ranged finisher | Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 | Heavy Crossbow Charged Ammunition, Blue Flame/Rending or Pure-Flame, Empowered blasts and ranged boss pressure |
| Hex/control flex | Stigmatized Witch 20 (Ember chassis) or Oracle 20 (Daeran chassis) | Evil Eye, Protective Luck, Cackle, enchantment/hex control, Heal and emergency support |

Do not run two competing rage songs. If Ember, Sosiel or another full caster is in the party, let that character decline Inspired Rage unless the current patch's Inciter capstone explicitly allows casting while raging.

## 1. Angel Oracle — 9.9/10 SS

**Main character:** **Seeker Oracle 16 / Scaled Fist Monk 1 / Paladin 2 / Hellknight 1 / Angel MR10**

This is the most forgiving all-purpose main. The merged Angel spellbook supplies long-duration defenses, healing, Angel bolts and high-end area damage. The Monk/Paladin/Hellknight levels add AC, saving throws and defensive stability.

| Slot | Build | Provides |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Main Angel Oracle above | Merged Angel casting, Sword of Heaven, bolts, Heal and party defenses |
| 2 | Inciter Skald 20 | Raises martial attack and damage, shares sneak dice and supplies AC/Will pressure |
| 3 | Brown-Fur Transmuter 20 | Shares size, Dexterity, Strength and weapon buffs with the front line |
| 4 | Cleric 20 | Guarded Hearth, communal Death Ward/True Seeing, healing and dispels |
| 5 | Sable Company Marine 1 / Paladin 13 / Sohei 1 / Mutation Warrior 5 | Mounted front line, smites, auras and target access |
| 6 | Stigmatized Witch 20 (Ember chassis) | Evil Eye, Protective Luck, Cackle and control |

**Opening:** pre-buff with the BFT and Cleric, place Guarded Hearth, start the Inciter song, then use an Angel bolt or area spell on the priority target. Heal only after the main threat has been removed.

## 2. Lich caster — 9.8/10 SS

**Main character:** **Stigmatized Witch 10 / Loremaster 9 / Crossblooded Sorcerer 1 / Lich MR10**

The merged Lich spellbook provides Corrupt Magic, negative-energy damage, strong debuffs and high-end control. It is more offensive and less forgiving than Angel, so the party must protect the caster and remove enemy defenses quickly.

| Slot | Build | Provides |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Main Lich caster above | Merged Lich casting, Corrupt Magic, Exsanguinate/Negative Eruption and control |
| 2 | Inciter Skald 20 | Improves the physical party and adds dispel/control pressure |
| 3 | Brown-Fur Transmuter 20 | Gives the two front liners the Strength, size, speed and weapon package |
| 4 | Cleric 20 | Guarded Hearth, communal defenses, Death Ward, Heal and emergency dispels |
| 5 | Sable Company Marine 1 / Paladin 13 / Sohei 1 / Mutation Warrior 5 | Mounted second front line, smites, auras and target access |
| 6 | Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 | Heavy Crossbow finisher; use Ember Witch 20 instead if more hex control is needed |

**Opening:** use BFT and Cleric defenses before entering, then use Corrupt Magic on the boss. Follow with Exsanguinate, Negative Eruption or the strongest save-based Lich spell. Do not let the Lich spend the first round on small healing.

## 3. Kinetic Sharpshooter — 9.7/10 SS

**Main character:** **Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 / Trickster MR10**

This is the independent ranged finisher. It is already the user's current core. The main requirement is a support shell that keeps enemies away and makes the melee party accurate enough to matter.

| Slot | Build | Provides |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Main Kinetic Sharpshooter above | Heavy Crossbow Charged Ammunition, Empowered Blue Flame, Rending/Pure-Flame and Trick Fate boss turns |
| 2 | Inciter Skald 20 | Shared sneak dice, Lethal Stance, Beast Totem and enemy AC/Will pressure |
| 3 | Brown-Fur Transmuter 20 | Greater Magic Weapon, Haste, Legendary Proportions/Transformation and concealment |
| 4 | Cleric 20 | Guarded Hearth, communal defenses, Death Ward, Freedom of Movement and healing |
| 5 | Mutation Warrior 19 / Demonslayer Ranger 1 | Mutagen, weapon training, Outflank and bonus damage against demons |
| 6 | Stigmatized Witch 20 (Ember chassis) or Oracle 20 (Daeran chassis) | Evil Eye, Protective Luck, Cackle or Heal; keep the KSS at range |

**Opening:** start the Inciter song, use **Trick Fate → Empowered Charged Ammunition**, and choose Blue Flame + Rending Arrows without problematic SR or Pure-Flame when SR is the problem. Use Chain Arrows only for lines or groups. Keep the KSS at range and do not spend turns chasing flanks.

## 4. Trickster Sword Saint — 9.7/10 SS

**Main character:** **Sword Saint 20 / Trickster MR10**

Sword Saint solves very high AC through Dimension Strike and Arcane Accuracy, then converts the Trickster critical chain and Trick Fate into a decisive full attack. It is a melee carry, so defensive buffs are mandatory.

| Slot | Build | Provides |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Main Sword Saint above | Arcane Pool, Dimension Strike, Prescient Attack, defensive wizard spells and Trickster critical feats |
| 2 | Inciter Skald 20 | Flanking, shared sneak dice and attack/damage support for both melee characters |
| 3 | Brown-Fur Transmuter 20 | Legendary Proportions/Transformation, Haste, GMW and Dexterity/Strength sharing |
| 4 | Cleric 20 | Guarded Hearth, communal defenses, Heal and dispels |
| 5 | Sable Company Marine 1 / Paladin 13 / Sohei 1 / Mutation Warrior 5 | Mounted second front line and flanking body |
| 6 | Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 or Stigmatized Witch 20 | Ranged pressure or extra hex/control while the Sword Saint is in melee |

**Opening:** cast or pre-apply Shield, Mirror Image and Displacement, then use **Arcane Pool → Dimension Strike → Trick Fate → full attack**. Do not enter melee before the defensive layers are active; the Sword Saint can hit the boss but still dies quickly to an unmitigated True Strike full attack.

## 5. Inciter Skald — 9.6/10 SS support

**Main character:** **Inciter Skald 20 / Trickster MR10**

Inciter is the party engine rather than the party's main damage dealer. Its value is highest when two or more martial characters can accept the song and attack every round.

| Slot | Build | Provides |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Main Inciter above | Inspired Rage/Raging Song, shared rage powers, shared sneak dice, dispel talents and enchantment control |
| 2 | Sword Saint 20 | Dimension Strike, Prescient Attack and a second high-accuracy melee full attack |
| 3 | Brown-Fur Transmuter 20 | Makes the martial core hit harder and survive longer through shared transformations |
| 4 | Cleric 20 | Guarded Hearth, communal defenses, healing and condition removal |
| 5 | Mutation Warrior 19 / Demonslayer Ranger 1 | Primary melee striker with mutagen, Outflank and demon damage |
| 6 | Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 | Ranged boss finisher that stays outside the song's melee cluster |

**Opening:** pre-buff first, activate Raging Song with Accept Rage only on the martial characters, then use the Inciter's dispel or selective control. The KSS shoots the priority target while both front liners full-attack. Ember, Sosiel and other casters should decline Rage unless the current patch confirms that they can still cast under the Inciter capstone.

## Fresh Legend note

The only two complete fresh IE Legend adaptations in this group are **Kinetic Sharpshooter 20 / Mutation Warrior 20** and **Inciter Skald 20 / Mutation Warrior 20**. They start at MR3 and reach character level 40. Angel Oracle, Lich caster and Trickster Sword Saint above are MR10-only systems in IE; they are not valid fresh Legend entries.
