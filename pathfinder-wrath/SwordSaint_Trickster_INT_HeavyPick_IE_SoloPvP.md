# Sword Saint 20 / Trickster MR10 — INT Heavy Pick Solo 1v1

## Build and duel rules

Human Sword Saint 20, Intelligence level-ups, Death's Consonant Heavy Pick, an empty off-hand, and Trickster MR10. Take Perception II, Knowledge (World) III, Lore (Religion) II with **Luck + Travel**, and Use Magic Device III.

Follow one natural level-1-to-20 progression and remain Trickster. This route uses all DLC, including Lord of Nothing. It does not require Hilor retraining or Toybox feat grants.

The duel allows normal self-buffs, the character's legal summons or legally acquired pet, and a declared finite inventory of rods, scrolls, and consumables. Summons and pets may attack and use their own abilities. No external teammates, paused buff timers, unlimited resource loops, or duplicated unique equipment are assumed. Use turn-based combat, equal player-character stat rules, and a start approximately 10 metres from the opponent; reaching melee is part of the opening. This class/domain route does not itself grant an animal companion.

This is a mechanically checked build and conditional duel plan, **not a live-tested PvP result or a guaranteed winner**. Implementation checks used the installed blueprint archive and English localization from Steam build **20212986**. The particular mask/teleport interactions still needing an in-game turn-based test are identified below.

Death's Consonant uses INT for attack and damage. Its base +3 enhancement can be raised to +5 with Greater Magic Weapon or Arcane Weapon; these do not add together to +8. The INT property remains.

Before acquiring that weapon, this character does not have INT-to-weapon-attack conversion. Use legal early Heavy Picks and the Magus control/defense book; the low-STR campaign opening is weaker than the finished item-based setup. Restore the **Branch of the Last Ash** crusade relic into the **Heavy Pick** version of Death's Consonant rather than the Bardiche version. A fresh IE character needs the actual item through its available legal inventory.

Progression gates:

- Dispel Focus and Greater Dispel Focus supply a combined **+4 on caster-level-based dispel checks**. Greater Dispel Focus follows its prerequisite at level 5. Both are ordinary feats here; neither consumes a Sword Saint combat bonus-feat slot. Their blueprint DLC condition requires the corresponding all-DLC unlock.
- Lunge requires BAB6, which this progression meets by level 9. Its active buff adds **5 feet of reach at a -2 AC cost**. A Medium heavy-pick user normally reaches approximately 10 feet with it, before other size/reach effects. This does not bridge a 10-metre start by itself.
- Fighter Training at Sword Saint 7 counts as Fighter level 4 for Weapon Specialization. At Sword Saint 17 it supplies effective Fighter14 for Greater Weapon Specialization.
- Select MR4's mythic critical feat only after learning ordinary Improved Critical at level 11. If MR4 unlocks first, delay that mythic selection until the ordinary prerequisite is present.
- Obtain **Perception II at MR4 before selecting the level 13 Trickster critical feat**.
- Obtain **Knowledge (World) III naturally at MR7 before selecting Greater Blind Fight at level 19**. Its prerequisite bypass permits skipping Improved Blind Fight. If class XP arrives earlier, delay that level-up until the required mythic feature is present; do not insert it retroactively.
- Greater Blind Fight is the fixed final feat. Its own components ignore partial concealment, treat total concealment as partial, and reroll melee concealment misses. Ordinary 50% total concealment can therefore become a 20% check with a reroll, leaving a 4% miss chance under the normal independent-roll rules. This is not automatic pinpointing or a demonstrated bypass of every special concealment or mask interaction.
- Stunning Critical is omitted: it first needs a critical-vulnerable target, then its stun/stagger can meet Fiery Body or Boots of Perfect Grounding. Greater Blind Fight improves usable weapon pressure against a broader prepared pool. No critical rider is selected, so Critical Focus, Staggering Critical and Critical Mastery do not consume slots.

The three changed slots serve the duel: Focused Strike only removes Power Attack's penalty from the first compatible two-handed attack; Sword Saint 11 already adds INT to the attack-of-opportunity allowance through Superior Reflexes; and Sword Saint 20 Weapon Mastery automatically confirms chosen-weapon critical threats, making Critical Focus's confirmation bonus redundant at the endpoint. Extra attacks of opportunity still require an enemy action that actually provokes.

Spell Specialization: Greater Dispel Magic and Destructive Dispel are **not selected** in this fixed progression. Spell Specialization offers another +2 spell caster levels but costs a feat and requires a legal spell selection; it is not included in the listed +4 dispel bonus. Destructive Dispel first needs a successful targeted dispel, then a Fortitude save for stun, with a sickened result on a successful save. Fiery Body also grants stun immunity. This route prioritizes getting a dispel through and reaching the opponent over adding that conditional rider.

Rupture Restraints is not the fixed choice. Flawless Headband supplies permanent Mind-Affecting immunity, Blind Fight addresses gazes, and the selected boots protect against stun/stagger. Perception II unlocks the special critical-feat chain. World III is acquired during progression, not supplied by a final respec. UMD III grants the Wizard book used for high-level self-preparation.

Luck and Travel are the two fixed Religion II domains. Luck supplies its normal luck tools; Travel supplies movement and Dimensional Hop. The current Domain Zealot blueprint explicitly accepts Trickster Religion II and includes Dimensional Hop among its affected abilities. Taking Religion II at MR8 makes that prerequisite available before MR9's generic mythic selection.

Dimensional Hop normally uses a **move action**. With Domain Zealot it uses a **swift action**, preserving the full-round action for an attack after teleporting into reach. That swift action is then unavailable for Dimension Strike, Arcane Accuracy, Prescient Attack, Arcane Weapon activation, or a quickened spell. Hop has a finite domain resource and a legal destination/range; do not turn it into unlimited movement. Domain actions, durations, uses, and spell access must be counted separately from Magus actions and spell slots.

**Archmage Armor timing:** this fixed arcana route does not supply native Mage Armor before UMD III. Use ordinary available defenses before MR10. Once the Wizard book is available, learn and self-cast Mage Armor. A Mage Armor potion or scroll does not activate Archmage Armor, and Bracers of Armor do not stack with its armor bonus.

### Duration and resource checks

Cast minutes-per-level protections first. Create the finite summon group late enough for its real duration to cover the fight, then apply compatible allied buffs.

This progression has **no Enduring Spells or Greater Enduring Spells**. Record the real remaining durations of every protection, weapon enhancement, and summon. Use finite Extend-rod charges where useful; do not replace normal durations with 24 hours. The Sword Saint and unmodified UMD III Wizard books are not CL30 merged books. Determine each cast's actual caster level from its own book and the equipment worn when casting.

Cast short defenses and **Trick Fate last**. Trick Fate's base duration is three rounds and its blueprint permits Extend; a suitable Greater Extend rod can make that six rounds. Preparation time and movement consume the remaining window. Finite rods and daily abilities retain their normal limits. Return to Death's Consonant before starting the duel if a casting weapon was used during preparation.

Do not use Transformation in the default preparation: it blocks ordinary casting and relevant spell-item use, removing the dispel and Wizard-book options needed in this duel plan.

## Opening and combat

### Initiative and the action budget

Apply the legal defensive package, finite summon screen, and weapon enhancement, then cast Trick Fate last. Sword Saint 19 fixes the initiative die at 20; compare the actual total with an opponent who also fixes that die. Winning initiative is not assumed. Give summons and pets their own turns without assuming they automatically hit, block teleports, or interrupt defensive casting.

A normal move or the base **move-action** Dimensional Hop leaves a standard action, not a full attack. A **swift** Domain Zealot Hop leaves the full-round attack available, but uses that turn's one swift action. At the stated starting distance, select a destination inside the weapon's actual reach; Lunge's extra 5 feet does not eliminate the approach.

Razmir's Mask supplies the first eligible **Spell-type** cast in a round as a **free action**. It does not simply quicken it and does not provide unlimited free casts. Spend the intended free spell before other abilities: the mask's implementation also removes its round marker on ability use. A second ordinary spell still needs its own action. The swift action remains separate and can pay for either Hop, a pool ability, or a quickened spell.

A quickened Trick Fate can use a suitable Greater Quicken rod charge because the spell is level 7. This spends the swift action. With the mask's free cast available, prefer the free-action cast when that is the required spell for the turn. Without either method, casting Trick Fate costs a standard action.

### Dispel checks and critical immunity

Bone Shield and Fiery Body each independently grant critical immunity. **Both must be gone before treating the target as critical-vulnerable**, unless inspection shows that only one was present. Fiery Body also grants stun immunity. Natural 20, automatic confirmation, Dimension Strike and critical riders do not override these immunities. Weapon attacks can still deal their applicable ordinary damage while critical immunity remains.

Use **Spell Combat off** for an ordinary weapon full attack. The empty off-hand permits the one-handed Heavy Pick's two-handed use; apply the actual hand-count/Power Attack calculation shown in the combat log. Enabling a legal Magus Spell Combat sequence changes the hand/action conditions and applies its attack penalty; it does not add a free Wizard-book spell to an ordinary full attack.

For this build's caster-level-based dispel checks, use:

`d20 + actual spell caster level + 4 from the two dispel feats + other verified check bonuses`

Compare that result with **11 + the individual enemy buff's caster level**. Active Trick Fate explicitly includes the DispelMagic rule and therefore supplies 20 for this die; a 20 is not an automatic success on this check. Use the actual book and casting equipment. The listed +4 is a check bonus, not another four true caster levels or a bonus to the spell's removal count.

Targeted Greater Dispel uses a finite allowance of **one removed effect per four actual spell caster levels**, trying effects with higher caster levels first and using one roll for the target's effects. It does not let the player nominate Bone Shield or Fiery Body. Other protections may consume the successful removals first. Check the combat log and remaining effects after every cast. Area Greater Dispel is a different mode with a smaller per-creature removal allowance; do not describe it as the same complete strip.

**Spell Turning is a separate gate before relying on any dispel result.** An enemy wearing Razmir's Mask may turn a targeted Greater Dispel back on this character. The mask's turning blueprint is not restricted to SR-affected spells, so Greater Dispel's or Corrupt Magic's SR:no entry does not establish a bypass. The exact targeted, area, self-cast, and two-mask interactions need current in-game verification. Do not assume an area cast safely bypasses turning either.

### Turn lines against a critical-immune, masked Lich

The table assumes this character still has its legal preparations, Trick Fate has time remaining, and the mask's free cast is available. These are conditional actions, not promised successful attacks or removals.

| Turn / observed state | Free spell | Swift action | Standard / move / full-round actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turn 1; enemy turning has not been shown safe to target | A needed self-protection, or preserve resources | Domain Zealot Hop into a legal attack position if committing | **Full-round:** normal full attack, resolving ordinary damage while critical immunity remains. If the risk is unacceptable, use positioning and the summon screen instead of committing |
| Turn 1 alternative; current testing establishes that the targeted dispel can reach this opponent | Targeted Greater Dispel; inspect reflection and actual removals | Domain Zealot Hop into reach | **Full-round:** full attack. There is no Dimension Strike this turn; surviving critical immunity still applies |
| Turn 2; already in reach, with a verified usable dispel route and critical protection remaining | Another targeted Greater Dispel | Dimension Strike if needed to hit the actual target | **Full-round:** full attack; critical damage is available only after all relevant immunity has actually ended |
| Turn 2 or later; Trick Fate needs renewal instead | Trick Fate | Dimension Strike if staying in reach, or Hop if repositioning | **Full-round:** attack only when safely in reach. This turn has no free Greater Dispel as well |
| Later turn; critical protection is gone and Trick Fate still covers the attack window | The most useful available spell, if any | Dimension Strike only if it improves the actual attack resolution | **Full-round:** full attack; count enemy Last Stand and other protections before expecting a finish |

If the first dispel reflects, abandon the assumption that repeating it will open the critical window. Reassess the lost self-buffs, use applicable ordinary weapon damage or another legal offense, and let the finite summons act. Spell Turning resolution is a required matchup check, not a reason to claim the heavy-pick critical plan always works.

Without Razmir's Mask, the conservative approach is **standard Greater Dispel -> move into position**, or **swift Domain Hop -> full attack** when spending that turn attacking is preferable to dispelling. Do not combine a standard-action dispel with an ordinary full attack. A legal Magus Spell Combat sequence is a separate option with its own requirements, not permission to add a Wizard-book cast to that line.

### Supported mask / Walk through Space interaction: runtime test pending

The installed `WalkThroughSpaceBuffAbility` is a **Spell-type** ability with a normal **move action**, while the mask's free-action component accepts Spell-type abilities without a specific-spell whitelist. That supports the reported interaction in which the first Walk through Space teleport each round uses the mask's free cast. **The combined turn-based behavior has not been tested here.**

If validated, pre-cast Walk through Space permits **free teleport -> swift Dimension Strike -> full attack**. Its teleport reaches up to 30 feet to an unoccupied, visible, standable destination, enough to close most of a 10-metre start and enter pick reach. This first free cast has already been spent: do not also add a free Greater Dispel or Trick Fate to that turn.

If the interaction does not work, use the confirmed domain action structure: **swift Domain Zealot Hop -> full attack**, forgoing Dimension Strike that turn. Ordinary Walk through Space teleportation instead costs a move action, and its underlying spell buff can be dispelled. Both routes still require a legal teleport destination and sufficient resources or remaining buff duration.

### Recovery after enemy Corrupt Magic or another dispel

Corrupt Magic belongs to the opponent's Lich book. It attempts to remove spell buffs and adds penalties according to its successful removals; it does not need this character to fail a saving throw. Trick Fate's 20 on saves therefore does not stop it. The own-mask reflection interaction is potentially valuable but is not an unconditional defense established by this guide.

| Recovery turn | Available action line | Decision being made |
|---|---|---|
| First own turn after the enemy cast | Inspect removed buffs, current penalties, controls, and Last Stand time; this fixed build has no Rupture Restraints recovery. **Free:** restore the most necessary spell, often Trick Fate if lost. **Swift:** Hop to a legal safe or attacking position | Full-attack only if position and surviving defenses permit it; otherwise use the remaining **standard and move** for further protection and spacing |
| Following own turn if the fight continues | **Free:** another essential protection or a verified safe Greater Dispel. **Swift:** Dimension Strike if remaining in range, or Hop if moving. **Full-round:** attack only when the preceding choices leave that action available | Restore what the next exchange needs; a single free spell cannot replace the complete lost preparation package |
| If the enemy also has Last Stand | Maintain a legal attack, defense, or repositioning line as its real protection window elapses | A large critical hit does not end the duel through Last Stand, and this character's own Last Stand is not permanent survival |

If the mask is unavailable, rebuilding a spell normally takes a standard action and leaves a move action. A Greater Quicken charge can pay for a swift spell instead, but then Hop and Dimension Strike are unavailable that turn. If Walk through Space was stripped, its teleport is gone until recast; Domain Hop remains a class/domain ability rather than that spell buff.

A normal full attack is not Spell Combat. Spell Combat is a separate full-round sequence using an eligible Magus spell, its free-hand requirement, and its normal attack penalty. A standalone True Strike normally uses a standard action; if legally included in Spell Combat, it benefits only the relevant first attack rather than every attack. Concealment, target immunities, and other remaining defenses still resolve normally.
