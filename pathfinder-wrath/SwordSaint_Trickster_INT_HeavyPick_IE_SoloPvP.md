# Sword Saint 20 / Trickster MR10 — INT Heavy Pick Solo 1v1

## Build and duel rules

Human Sword Saint 20, Intelligence level-ups, Death's Consonant Heavy Pick, an empty off-hand, and Trickster MR10. Take Perception II, Knowledge (World) III, Lore (Religion) II with **Luck + Travel**, and Use Magic Device III.

Follow one natural level-1-to-20 progression and remain Trickster. This route uses all DLC, including Lord of Nothing. It does not require Hilor retraining or Toybox feat grants.

The duel allows normal self-buffs, the character's legal summons, and a declared finite inventory of rods, scrolls, and consumables. Summons may attack and use their own abilities. No external teammates, paused buff timers, unlimited resource loops, or duplicated unique equipment are assumed. Start approximately 10 metres from the opponent; reaching melee is part of the opening.

Death's Consonant uses INT for attack and damage. Its base +3 enhancement can be raised to +5 with Greater Magic Weapon or Arcane Weapon; these do not add together to +8. The INT property remains.

Increase **INT** at levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 20, reaching INT24 before equipment and permanent rewards. INT also supports Canny Defense, Lightning Draw initiative, Critical Perfection, Arcane Pool, and casting. Lightning Draw does not exist at level 1; follow the class progression.

Prioritize Use Magic Device, Knowledge (World), Knowledge (Arcana), Perception, and Lore (Religion). Spend additional ranks on Trickery and Stealth; this build does not invest in a demoralize package. Domain features still use the ability scores and resource limits stated in their actual descriptions.

Progression gates:

- Focused Strike requires STR13 and Power Attack. It removes Power Attack's penalty from the first attack of the turn when a compatible weapon is held in two hands. It does not remove penalties from all iterative attacks or attacks of opportunity. Spell Combat requires its own free-hand mode; do not assume that mode also grants the two-handed benefit.
- Fighter Training at Sword Saint 7 counts as Fighter level 4 for Weapon Specialization. At Sword Saint17 it supplies effective Fighter14 for Greater Weapon Specialization.
- Critical Focus cannot be taken at level3. Critical Perfection at Sword Saint9 permits Magus level to satisfy the applicable critical-feat BAB requirement.
- Select MR4's mythic critical feat only after learning ordinary Improved Critical at level11. If MR4 unlocks first, delay that mythic selection until the ordinary prerequisite is present.
- Obtain **Perception II at MR4 before selecting the level13 Trickster critical feat**.
- Obtain **Knowledge (World) III naturally at MR7 before selecting Stunning Critical at level19**. This is the reason the route can omit Staggering Critical. If class XP arrives earlier, delay that level-up until the required mythic feature is present; do not insert it retroactively.
- Stunning Critical is the only critical rider. It already has a staggered result on a successful Fortitude save. Critical Mastery would need a second usable rider to add a second effect, so neither Critical Mastery nor Staggering Critical is selected.

Luck and Travel are the two fixed Religion II domains. Luck supplies its normal luck tools; Travel supplies movement and Dimensional Hop. Domain actions, durations, uses, and spell access must be counted separately from Magus actions and spell slots.

**Archmage Armor timing:** this fixed arcana route does not supply native Mage Armor before UMD III. Use ordinary available defenses before MR10. Once the Wizard book is available, learn and self-cast Mage Armor. A Mage Armor potion or scroll does not activate Archmage Armor, and Bracers of Armor do not stack with its armor bonus.

## Spellbooks and preparation

The Sword Saint book and the UMD III Wizard book remain separate. Copy legitimate scrolls into the Wizard book and prepare/rest before the duel. A Wizard-book spell is not automatically eligible for Magus Spell Combat.

### Sword Saint spell priorities

| Spell level | Main preparation |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Shield; True Strike; optional Shocking Grasp |
| 2 | Mirror Image; Blur; Frigid Touch |
| 3 | Haste; Displacement; Greater Magic Weapon |
| 4 | Dimension Door; Greater Invisibility; Stoneskin |
| 5 | Vampiric Shadow Shield; useful utility or control |
| 6 | True Seeing; Greater Dispel Magic; Walk through Space |

Touch spells are optional utility, not the primary damage investment. A spell still needs its normal attack, SR, save, and immunity checks.

### Wizard-book priorities at MR10

- Mage Armor, Shield, Mirror Image, and Greater Magic Weapon.
- Mind Blank, Foresight, Protection from Spells, and appropriate sensory defenses.
- Greater Heroism or Heroic Invocation; their morale bonuses overlap rather than add.
- Greater Dispel Magic, including spare copies.
- Dimension Door and suitable control options.
- Legal Summon Monster spells and other chosen summons.
- Appropriate energy protections.

Freedom of Movement and Death Ward come from legal external divine consumables or the actual domain resources available to this build. They are not ordinary Wizard-book spells.

### Duration and resource checks

Cast minutes-per-level protections first. Create the finite summon group late enough for its real duration to cover the fight, then apply compatible allied buffs.

Greater Enduring Spells does not automatically make every rounds-per-level spell last a day. Such a spell normally needs **CL25 with Extend** to reach the required duration. The unmodified UMD III book is CL20; Robe of the Seven Sins alone does not reach CL25. Use an actual owned casting setup and an appropriate Extend rod if relying on that threshold, then return to Death's Consonant before combat.

Cast short defenses and **Trick Fate last**. Trick Fate's base duration is three rounds; preparation time and movement consume the window. Finite rods and daily abilities retain their normal limits.

Do not use Transformation in the default preparation: it blocks ordinary casting and relevant spell-item use, removing the dispel and Wizard-book options needed in this duel plan.

## Opening and combat

1. Apply the legal defensive package, summon screen, and weapon enhancement.
2. Cast Trick Fate immediately before the duel and record its remaining duration.
3. Check initiative and the distance to the enemy. Sword Saint19's initiative feature fixes the initiative die at20; compare total bonuses with other fixed-20 characters.
4. Reach attack range using a legal movement/action sequence. At a 10-metre start, a normal move generally prevents a stationary full attack that turn.
5. Full-attack with Death's Consonant when the full-round action is available. Use the Arcane Pool tools needed for the actual target.
6. After Trick Fate expires, **Dimension Strike (swift) → full attack** is the standard high-AC attack window when already in range.
7. Give summons their own actions. Use them to create pressure or screen approaches without assuming they automatically block teleports or land hits.

Trick Fate replaces relevant dice with20; it does not remove critical immunity, concealment, or every other defense. Do not automatically spend a swift action on Dimension Strike when the fixed-20 attack already hits, but evaluate Touch AC if an immunity to automatic hits or another special defense changes the result.

A quickened Trick Fate is possible with a suitable Greater Quicken Rod charge because it is level7. That uses the swift action, so it cannot share that turn's swift action with Dimension Strike or Prescient Attack. An ordinary in-combat cast uses the standard action.

A normal full attack is not Spell Combat. Spell Combat is a separate full-round sequence using an eligible Magus spell, its free-hand requirement, and its normal attack penalty. A standalone True Strike uses a standard action; if legally included in Spell Combat, it benefits only the relevant first attack rather than every attack.

## Matchup priorities

| Opponent / defense | Response |
|---|---|
| High ordinary AC | Compare fixed-20 hits with a Dimension Strike attack window |
| Concealment / invisibility | Use the appropriate sensory defense; do not assume True Seeing defeats every concealment source |
| Critical immunity, including Bone Shield | Remove dispellable protection or use another available offense; Trick Fate does not cancel critical immunity |
| High-CL merged caster | Preserve defenses and assess the actual dispel check; the UMD book does not become a CL30 merged book |
| Last Stand | Count its remaining protection window; a huge first hit does not finish through it automatically |
| Enemy summon army | Use positioning, your own summons, and available control or area options |
| Aeon dispelling | Expect temporary buffs to be removed and retain enough resources to act after the first exchange |

Corrupt Magic belongs to Lich, not this Sword Saint. This route has Luck and Travel, so do not assume it also owns a Magic-domain dispel ability. Trickery I uses its own check; Trickery II is not selected here.
