# Solo 1v1 Build Comparison — Self-Summons Allowed

## Current recommendation

**Default Lich: ordinary Wizard 20 / Divination / merged Lich MR10.**

No reviewed alternative has established a universal improvement over this configuration under the rules below. This is a first candidate for testing, not a proven champion against every optimized counter-build. Use the [complete natural progression](./WOTR_Lich_Complete_Endgame_Build_EN.md).

The former decimal scores and supposed matchup averages have been removed. No round-robin experiment supports those numbers. PvE damage rankings do not establish a PvP order.

## Rules

- All DLC; level 20 / MR10. A fresh Legend has its actual legal level-40 progression.
- Turn-based combat; approximately 10 metres between starting positions.
- No external party members. Normal class/path companions and legal self-summons may fight, including their own spells and abilities.
- Summoning, buffs, charges, daily abilities, and preparation time follow normal limits. Timers run throughout preparation and combat.
- No unlimited growth, duplicated unique equipment, infinite item/reset loops, imported enemy armies, or respec-based feat bypass. A normal path transition retains only what the game actually preserves; declare and verify any legitimately retained feats or merged spellbook.
- Use actual owned equipment and a declared finite consumable inventory. A DLC reward is not automatically owned by a fresh IE character.
- For an unknown opponent, fix equipment and prepared spells before revealing the matchup. Known-opponent preparation is a separate test; apply it equally to both sides.
- Short pre-buffs, including a Trickster's own Trick Fate, are allowed, but the real remaining duration at initiative must be recorded.
- Swarm requires a declared Swarm Size and finite clone/equipment allocation before comparison.

“Solo” describes the absence of external party assistance. It does not mean that a Lich must dismiss the Skeletal Champion or that an Azata must dismiss Aivu under the user's selected rules.

A body-only format excludes fighting companions, summons, clones, and similar extra units. Results from that format cannot simply be copied into this one.

## What changes when summons count?

1. Extra units can attack, cast their own spells, occupy space, and consume enemy actions. This can change a duel without improving the main character's damage number.
2. Lich gains value from the Skeletal Champion, controlled undead, and appropriate undead support. The skeleton is not a free second merged level-20 caster.
3. Azata must be assessed with Aivu's legal actions included; “there is only one enemy” no longer justifies dismissing the path's contribution.
4. Aeon summons may gain additional path support. Reports of implementation differences require checking the combat log; do not award an automatic army-wide dispel engine on description alone.
5. Trickster can use its real path summons and the Wizard book from UMD III. A hostile Perpetually Annoyed Wizard is not automatically a controllable ally.
6. Swarm's finite clones can alter its position substantially. A path able to reach five clones at Swarm Size 15 is a different test from a size-zero Swarm. No unique item is duplicated between clones by assumption.
7. Area attacks, crowd control, and targeting the summoner become more valuable. A body-only initiative-and-burst comparison is insufficient.

These changes do not establish a numerical promotion for every summoning build. In particular, the Lich spell/summon core is largely shared by Wizard archetypes; allowing those spells does not automatically make the former reservoir build preferable to Divination.

## Why Divination remains the default Lich

At ordinary Wizard 20, Divination supplies +10 school initiative and a fixed natural-20 initiative roll. The Hare bond and all five Wizard bonus-feat selections remain available. The class still merges with Lich and retains the normal Wizard/Lich spell and summon toolkit.

Shadowcaster 20 / Divination is a real alternative, especially when its own shadow participates. It retains the school but loses Arcane Bond and four bonus feats. Its +5 profane INT cannot simply be added to Nocticula's larger profane INT reward. Old claims about the shadow rebelling are not a sound current-version reason to reject it.

Scroll Savant 20 / Divination is another real alternative when a specified finite scroll inventory matters. Its scroll benefits must be compared with its lost bonus feats and the resources every other caster may also legally use. Scroll Savant 10 / Loremaster 10 does not obtain the Wizard-20 school capstone.

Neither alternative has been demonstrated to dominate the complete ordinary-Diviner configuration across this candidate pool. The default therefore changes to ordinary Divination Wizard without claiming that every possible opponent has been solved.

## Matchup checks

### Lich versus Trickster Sword Saint

Bone Shield and Fiery Body can supply critical immunity. Trick Fate fixes the Trickster's relevant dice; it does not remove that immunity.

Corrupt Magic has no target saving throw and no SR check, but still needs the relevant dispel checks. The target's Trick Fate does not replace the Lich caster's dispel dice. A dispel does not automatically remove permanent class/path features.

An active Trick Fate also affects the Trickster's saves. Do not assume Absolute Death's failed-save result against it. The successful-save damage branch, defensive immunities, available healing, and Last Stand all need separate accounting.

The Sword Saint has its own fixed-20 initiative at class level 19 and adds INT through Lightning Draw. Divination's fixed die is not proof that the Wizard always acts first.

### Aeon

Zero State supplies a substantial dispel-check bonus, but range, Selective compatibility, and action cost still matter. At a nominal 10-metre separation, a roughly 30-foot caster-centred effect may require repositioning.

Edict of Invulnerability is not universal immunity to every damage type. A theoretical counter must still show a legal sequence that survives and acts.

### Swarm

Declare Swarm Size, clone count, finite resources, and equipment for each unit. Merely allowing summons does not authorize unlimited size farming or duplication of equipped unique rewards.

Physical immunity is not a complete answer to every weapon user if a legal effect changes damage to force. Conversely, the existence of a force conversion is not proof that it bypasses every other Swarm defense. Check the actual version and combat log.

### Other Lich variants

Examine initiative, save DC, dispelling, protections, and spell availability separately. A higher single-spell DC is not the same as an established better average against unknown optimized opponents.

## Action and resource accounting

- A normal turn supplies one standard, one move, and one swift action. A normal full attack uses the standard/move package.
- A Sword Saint starting 10 metres away cannot assume a stationary full attack without a demonstrated way to reach attack range while preserving that action.
- Swift arcana, a swift quickened spell, and another swift setup compete for the same swift action.
- A Greater Quicken Rod normally works through spell level 9. It does not quicken Absolute Death at level 10.
- If a legitimately obtained Razmir's Mask grants its first-spell action reduction, count that free-action spell once, plus the remaining normal actions. The mask does not grant extra swift actions.
- Razmir's Mask and Razmir's Ring are alternative normal quest rewards. Do not grant one character both by assumption.
- Deadly Magic and rods have finite uses. Buffs do not pause while an army is summoned.
- Magic Nullification cannot be counted as unrestricted permanent protection while also retaining every removed magical buff.
- No claim of an automatic Last Stand bypass is assumed.

## Test record needed before declaring a champion

For each matchup, record the game version, both complete builds, gear and consumable inventories, starting positions, buff order and remaining durations, each additional unit, full initiative totals, actions, and combat-log results. Test both orientations and realistic preparation variants.

Until those comparisons exist, the honest conclusion is:

**Start with ordinary Divination Wizard 20 / merged Lich for the default Lich. Include summons in every matchup. Treat Aeon, Trickster Sword Saint, Demon, Angel, Azata, and a properly bounded Swarm as serious comparison targets rather than assigning unsupported decimal margins.**
