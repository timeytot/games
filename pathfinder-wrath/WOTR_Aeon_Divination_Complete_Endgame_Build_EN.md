# Divination Wizard 20 / Aeon MR10 — Natural Solo 1v1 Build

## Scope and fixed configuration

Use **Elf, ordinary Wizard 20, Divination, Hare familiar, Lawful Neutral Aeon MR10**. This is one natural advancement route, with finite self-summons and a declared equipment inventory. It does not use Hilor retraining, Toybox grants, inherited features from another mythic path, or a merged spellbook.

The benchmark is all DLC, Level 20 / MR10, turn-based combat, approximately 10 metres between the opposing main characters, equal player-character numerical rules, normal preparation, and normal duration consumption. Legal self-summons may participate; external teammates may not. Declare their starting positions on their controller's side. No precombat hostile actions, surrounding the opponent before initiative, repeated rest loops, duplicated unique equipment, or imported enemy armies are assumed.

The build is a serious prepared-caster challenger to Lich and the two Sword Saint paths. **It has not won a live-tested round robin and is not a demonstrated universal champion.** Its important advantages include independent Wizard and Aeon resources, Zero State, Perfect Form, defensive edicts, free-action gazes, and Greater Aeon's Bane action compression. Its important weaknesses include dispellable preparation, finite Bane rounds, short-range Zero State, targeted Spell Turning, and opponents whose immunity defeats a particular attack.

Reviewed on **2026-10-30** against installed Steam **build 20212986**, the local blueprint archive, English localization, and the action-handling code described in the evidence section. A blueprint-confirmed property and an untested combined combat interaction are distinguished below.

### Why these generic mythic slots?

- **No Abundant Casting chain:** the explicit Wizard preparation below fits the single duel. These abilities add endurance rather than actions. The inspected standalone-mythic spellbook code does not add ordinary casting-stat bonus slots or the generic extra-spells-per-day pool to Aeon's daily slots; do not invent four additional Zero State or Edict casts from Abundant Casting.
- **No Enduring Spells pair:** timed self-preparation and finite Extend resources cover the intended duel. Extending preparation to 24 hours would cost two mythic slots while not preventing an enemy dispel. Bane rounds and gazes are not ordinary spell buffs extended by that pair.
- **Ascendant Summons:** directly supports the permitted unit pressure. At MR10 its inspected buff adds +6 STR, DEX, CON, WIS, CHA and physical-damage bypass properties to eligible summons; there is no +6 INT component. Construct immunities can make a particular ability-score benefit irrelevant.
- **School Mastery: Abjuration:** increases the actual CL of Zero State and Greater Dispel Magic, rather than only improving a save DC that neither dispel uses.
- **Favorite Selective:** puts Selective Zero State in level 5 instead of 6 and Selective Supernova at its original level. It does not by itself remove spontaneous-metamagic casting-time rules.
- **Favorite Persistent:** reduces compatible Persistent control to +1 spell level. There is no benefit on a spell with no saving throw.
- **No automatic Rupture Restraints pick:** Aeon grants substantial permanent condition protection, reinforced by the listed equipment and Fiery Body. Residual control and dispelling still matter; the immunity list is not universal.

Self-cast **Mage Armor** to activate Archmage Armor. Its armor bonus does not add to another armor bonus from Bracers of Armor.

## The three important Aeon mechanics

### Zero State

Zero State is a **level-5 Aeon Abjuration spell**, with personal targeting and a **30-foot burst**. It has no saving throw and no SR check. The installed action explicitly **excludes the caster** before attempting dispels. Ordinary Zero State can still strip allied summons and other allies in the burst; Selective protects those recipients where its actual exclusion applies.

Its dispel action is uncapped, adds **+10 to the check**, attempts multiple spell-origin effects, and uses **separate rolls**, not one shared die. It does not remove every permanent item/class/mythic feature, and a numerical dispel check is not an attack or saving throw with automatic success on 20.

With the declared robe, staff, school mastery, and an active Caster Level gaze:

| Component | Zero State |
|---|---:|
| Independent Aeon book at MR10 | 20 CL |
| Robe of the Seven Sins | +3 CL |
| Sin Mage's Staff | +3 CL |
| School Mastery: Abjuration | +1 CL |
| Caster Level gaze, currently active | +4 CL |
| **Actual spell CL** | **31** |
| Zero State built-in check bonus | +10 |
| Dispel Focus + Greater Dispel Focus | +4 |
| **Dispel check** | **1d20 +45** |

Against a buff actually cast at CL41, the normal dispel DC is **52**, so the example needs a die result of **7**, not an automatic success. Without the gaze the spell has CL27 and checks at **1d20 +41**. Do not add the built-in +10 or the focus +4 to buff duration, dispel resistance, or real CL.

Greater Dispel Magic gains its own +2 Spell Specialization and therefore reaches **CL33, check 1d20 +37** in the same active-gaze example. It does not receive Zero State's +10. The targeted version uses its actual removal limit and shared-roll behavior; it is not an unrestricted substitute for Zero State's multi-check burst.

**Foretell — Aid** is an additional finite Divination-school tool, not a gaze. It activates as a **standard action** in its blueprint and spends rounds from a pool equal to Wizard level: twenty at the endpoint. Its inspected allied buff includes an explicit **+2 dispel-check component**, in addition to the described luck/penetration support. If it was legally activated during preparation and remains in effect, the two examples become **1d20 +47** and **1d20 +39** respectively. The CL figures do not change. Pay the activation time and elapsed rounds, and use the actual log rather than multiplying every +2 through a ring. Neither the main table nor the initiative example silently includes this finite additional support.

### Greater Aeon's Bane

Aeon's Bane is a **swift activation**, with **20 rounds per rest at MR10** for this Wizard. Count preparation rounds if it is activated early. While the relevant Greater Bane buff remains active, the installed engine's action handling converts an available **standard** non-Physical ability to a **move** action when the move-action budget is unused. It is not restricted to spells from the Aeon book: a Wizard spell can qualify too.

This allows **one move-action spell and one standard-action spell**, alongside one swift action, under the actual action conditions. It does not create a second move, a second swift, or unlimited standard actions. Cast the intended move-action spell before spending the move budget on walking. If movement already spent it, the next ordinary spell retains its standard cost.

The full-round check in the inspected engine is conditioned on the spell still being a standard action. That supports a converted summon or spontaneous-metamagic cast using move instead, but verify its actual current turn-based action display before relying on the combined conversion. The normal-cost fallback is provided below.

Bane also supplies its actual weapon/penetration bonuses and on-hit dispel effects. The inspected base buff's dispel trigger is tied to successful **attack rolls**, so do not claim that Magic Missile, every area pulse, or any spell merely cast automatically produces multiple Bane dispels. Ray attacks can provide qualifying attack-roll opportunities if they hit and resolve. The Greater buff also carries a no-check one-effect weapon-hit dispel component; its current first-hit/per-round implementation and summons' inherited upgrade behavior need a live combat-log check before being multiplied into a guaranteed strip-everything sequence.

At MR6 the summon-support component lets eligible summons benefit from Aeon's Bane while the owner uses it. This is a reason to retain summon pressure, not permission to give every unit an independently unlimited Bane pool or duplicate the caster's exact attack bonus.

### Edict of Invulnerability and final immunities

**Edict of Invulnerability** is a level-7 Aeon spell cast as a **swift action**, applying its two component buffs to allies in 30 feet: immunity to the three physical damage forms and suppression of the specified negative conditions. The duration is **one round per five actual CL**, rounded by the spell's progression. At the CL31 illustration it is **6 rounds**; before the gaze, CL27 gives **5 rounds**. A valid Extend resource can increase a compatible duration, but spends its charge and does not stop the timer.

It is not immunity to force, divine, fire, or every source of damage. Bane of Spirit's paid force conversion and separate non-physical riders must be evaluated. The suppression component is not a proof that every possible kill action, Spell Turning, or enemy dispel is negated. Swift Edict competes with swift Bane activation, rod-quickened casting, and other swift abilities.

The MR10 immunity description must not be read as universal immunity removal. The inspected components provide fixed-20 initiative, fixed-20 saving throws against chaotic sources, a fixed-20 SR check against chaotic targets, and the relevant damage-reduction bypass component. These are distinct from automatically bypassing all condition immunities, spell immunity, critical immunity, Last Stand, or a mask. **Do not add two initiative dice of 20:** Divination and Aeon's MR10 feature supply the same fixed die, not +20 each.

Mythic Suppression is restricted to its actual mythic-demon targets. A player using the Demon path is not automatically a qualifying scripted mythic demon, and a Lich, Trickster, or Swarm is not automatically susceptible. No matchup below assumes that this ability removes a player's mythic path.

### Against Sword Saint / Swarm, Size15 with up to five clones

Treat the main body and each legal clone as separate acting units with its declared equipment and spell/feature access. Do not infer that all six have Death's Consonant, Razmir's Mask, or Wind Breath. The Swarm's actual initiative may exceed the Aeon's; the first action cannot simply be awarded to this Wizard.

The inspected Swarm weapon-damage protection makes an ordinary physical summon **weapon attack** an insufficient default damage answer. A lone list of spell names is not a verified counter to all five clones.

Zero State can remove eligible cloned units' **spell buffs** within the burst. It does not dispel their permanent Swarm path features or delete clones merely because they exist. Summoner's Gaze must not be assumed to banish clones without confirming their summoned-unit classification. Against several mobile threats, use the actual reach of the three active gazes, protection windows, available healing, and remaining movement.
