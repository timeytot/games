# Melee Trickster Notes (General)

Generic reference for Magus / Sword Saint / Duelist characters on the Trickster mythic path, optionally finishing as Legend. No character-specific data.

## Trickster then Legend

### Main campaign

- Choose **Trickster** at Mythic Rank 3. **Legend** unlocks in Act 5 (normally around Mythic Rank 8) via story dialogue / checks.
- Common melee plan: Perception critical chain on Trickster, then convert to Legend at MR8. Some Trickster crit feats can be retained; most other mythic path powers are not.

### Inevitable Excess (new character)

- **Different rules.** Legend is only offered at the **MR3 path pick** (level 40 / mythic 3 start).
- You **cannot** play Trickster (or Angel/Lich/etc.) and convert to Legend at MR8 inside IE.
- Details and DLC5/DLC6 archetype notes: [IE_Legend_and_DLC_Archetypes.md](./IE_Legend_and_DLC_Archetypes.md).

## Mage Armor on Magus / Sword Saint

- **Mage Armor is not on the Magus spell list.** Missing it at level 1 spell selection is expected.
- **Spell Blending does not exist in WotR** (tabletop-only Magus arcana). Magus cannot add Wizard Mage Armor via arcana.
- Ways to get a self-cast Mage Armor for **Archmage Armor** (must be **you** casting on yourself after patch 2.2.0; ally cast / potions / items do not grant the mythic bonus):
  1. Dip a class that learns Mage Armor (commonly Stigmatized Witch 1).
  2. Otherwise skip Archmage Armor and use Shield, Mirror Image, Canny Defense, and gear.
- **Shield** is on the Magus list and stacks with Mage Armor for defense.

## Greater Mythic Tricks (Rank 3)

Source: [Fextralife — Mythic Trick](https://pathfinderwrathoftherighteous.wiki.fextralife.com/Mythic+Trick)

| Trick | Rank 3 effect |
|---|---|
| Athletics | BAB is at least your Athletics ranks; if BAB already equals character level, BAB is at least Athletics ranks + 5 |
| Knowledge (Arcana) | Identified items gain an extra random major enchantment |
| Knowledge (World) | You and allies may ignore feat prerequisites on level-up |
| Lore (Nature) | Hunting trips can return a random magic item |
| Lore (Religion) | Choose 2 more domains (up to 4 total with Rank 2) |
| Mobility | When hit, attempt a Mobility check at −10; if higher than the attack roll, the attack misses |
| Perception | Equip up to 2 unused Rank 1 tricks per day; reassign daily |
| Persuasion | Enemies that fail the demoralize save must Coup de Grace themselves on their first turn |
| Stealth | Spell resistance = Stealth bonus − 10 |
| Trickery | Attempt to “turn off” a creature; Fort DC 15 + Trickery ranks or die |
| Use Magic Device / Infuse Magic Device | Gain a wizard-like spellbook; copy spells from scrolls (Intelligence, caster level = character level) |

### Melee priority (general)

| Rank | Trick | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| High | Athletics 3 | Strong BAB fix for Magus BAB |
| High | Persuasion 3 | Strong if Persuasion ranks are invested |
| Core earlier | Perception 2 | Required for the Trickster critical feat chain; Rank 3 is optional |
| Situational | Knowledge (World) 3 | Party feat flexibility, especially before a Legend rebuild |
| Situational | Mobility 3 | Defensive |
| Low for pure melee | Arcana / Nature / Religion / Stealth / Trickery / UMD 3 | Utility or niche |

Most melee Trickster → Legend characters only need to secure **Perception 2** before the Legend swap. Extra Greater tricks are optional if Mythic Rank 7 still has room.

## Combat opener habits (melee Magus)

These matter more in short fights than unused skill tricks:

1. Pre-fight: Heroic Invocation (or equivalent party buffs), **Haste**, Mirror Image, Mage Armor, optional Greater Invisibility.
2. On engage: turn on **Spell Combat**, then a touch/spell-strike spell (for example Shocking Grasp), then full attacks.
3. **Piranha Strike** (if taken): on for trash; off if attack bonus feels low against elites.
4. Skill-based Mythic Tricks rarely matter inside a pure damage fight; do not spend mythic picks on them for that reason alone.

## Magus bonus feats

- Magus bonus feats arrive at Magus levels **5 / 11 / 17**.
- Not every combat feat appears on the Magus bonus list (for example Fencing Grace may need a regular feat slot).
- If you take **Fencing Grace** for Dexterity to damage, you usually do not also need Mythic Weapon Finesse for damage; Mythic Weapon Finesse still needs base Weapon Finesse if you want that mythic feat for other reasons.
