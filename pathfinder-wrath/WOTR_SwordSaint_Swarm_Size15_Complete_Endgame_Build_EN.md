# Sword Saint 20 / Swarm MR10 — Size 15, Five Clones, Solo 1v1

## Configuration and limits

**Human Sword Saint 20, Trickster until MR7, then Swarm-that-Walks at MR8; fixed Swarm Size 15; at most five clones.** The main character uses Death's Consonant Heavy Pick. Clones use separately acquired ordinary Heavy Picks, supported by their large Swarm Strength bonus. This is one natural progression, without Hilor retraining or Toybox feature grants.

Use the [shared solo-duel rules](../README.md): all DLC, level 20/MR10, turn-based combat, equal player-character numerical rules, approximately 10 metres between the main characters. Legal owned units participate. Declare all units, equipment, remaining resources and starting positions; clones start on their controller's side of the gap, with no precombat encirclement or hostile actions. Self-buff and preparation timers run normally.

**Size 15 is an entry requirement, not a reward automatically granted by MR10.** The nominal growth thresholds use 100 times the next size in devoured challenge ratings, totaling **12,000 CR** through the size15 threshold. The inspected growth code uses a strict threshold comparison, so do not assume an exact total of12,000 already displays Size15: the actual displayed size controls entry. Reach that size through actual permitted campaign encounters before entering this benchmark. A normal campaign ending is not a guarantee of this amount of growth. Do not edit the size, consume the same corpse repeatedly, or treat an unlimited growth loop as part of this build. Stop at Size15 for comparison.

The five-clone configuration is a serious top-tier candidate. It is not a demonstrated 96% win rate, a tested 96/100 result, or proof that every caster loses. Ordinary weapon immunity, conditional spell immunity, independent actions, dispelling, area damage and the actual equipment allocation all matter.

### Magic Nullification is a separate tactical state

The default preparation below leaves **Magic Nullification off**. Turning it on is a free activation, removes spell effects on that unit, and repeats spell-effect removal on subsequent rounds. Its installed immunity component is `AllSpells`, not merely ordinary SR. The readable engine implementation checks whether the delivered ability is a spell, rather than using the SR:yes flag as its sole gate.

Consequently, **SR:no alone does not establish a bypass of this toggle**. Conversely, its interaction with Lich Deadly Magic, Absolute Death, Corrupt Magic, Aeon special abilities and area contexts must be verified in the current combat log. Do not announce blanket invincibility or maintain the full self-buff sheet while the toggle is active. Native Swarm supernatural features and actual item properties are separate from the removed spell buffs, but verify the resulting attacks/resources in the UI before using this state as a decisive test case.

Use the toggle when the extra spell defense is worth sacrificing Haste, Mirror Image, Greater Invisibility, Walk through Space and other spell preparation. Different units may make that choice on their own turns; it is not an automatic interrupt of an enemy cast.

## Clone creation, equipment and resources

Create the permitted clones **before** final preparation. The clone ability is supernatural with **`m_IsFullRoundAction = true`**. Its condition branches create the currently available clone positions; confirm the actual number created rather than assigning a separate free summon every round. At Size15/MR10 there may be five clones in addition to the original, not five units total.

The inspected clone flow copies the character with **`copyItems:false`**, detaches all copied buffs, removes its own clone-spawning/swarm-management parts, removes the source clone ability, then adopts it as a clone pet. Therefore:

- **No item is copied, including a generic item.** Supply every Heavy Pick, belt, headband, cloak, ring and consumable from real inventory.
- **No prebuff snapshot is copied.** Create clones first, equip them, then prepare/buff each intended unit. They cannot inherit one spent scroll six times.
- They retain the copied class/learned-feat framework and use their own actions. The shared `SwarmScale` property reads the main character's swarm size, explaining why removing a clone's own swarm-growth part does not make its size bonus zero.
- Treat daily resources and prepared slots as separate unit state after creation. Inspect actual remaining slots, Arcane Pool, Quickened Magic and Last Stand availability; do not assume a newly created clone refills resources already spent by its source. One ordinary rest may establish the declared starting resource state. Repeated destroy/recreate or rest loops to manufacture more daily resources are excluded.
- Clones do not recursively create more clones. Six independently equipped bodies are the maximum configuration here.

### Against Divination Wizard / Lich

Use the initiative advantage and six independent weapon-action sources, but do not promise a first-round kill through Last Stand. **Bone Shield and Fiery Body keep their critical immunity until actually removed.** The main has no Corrupt Magic, and Greater Dispel Magic is weaker against a high merged CL than the Lich's own dispelling.

With Robe of the Seven Sins, main Magus CL is23 before other real modifiers; Dispel Focus plus Greater Dispel Focus produces **d20 +27** on applicable dispel checks. A clone without that robe has **d20 +24**. Against a buff at CL38, the DC49 exceeds the main's maximum47; the dispel cannot succeed just because a20 is rolled. Do not waste all six swift resources on an unreachable defense. Reserve dispelling for buffs with attainable DCs, or use ordinary damage and compatible supernatural pressure while tracking immunity and Last Stand windows.

Spread enough to reduce one area spell's coverage, while remaining within useful reach. A clone is not an automatic counterspell, and nonweapon physical area damage can bypass the weapon-protection component. Magic Nullification is a legitimate alternate defense to test; Deadly Magic and special kill/guard interactions remain a live-verification priority, not a proven Lich defeat.

### Against Divination Wizard / Aeon

Do not stack every body inside one optimal Zero State placement. An enemy can remove ordinary spell preparation without removing the source Sword Saint class, Swarm size or genuine equipped item properties. Check which supernatural/path buffs the actual effect can remove instead of assuming universal dispellability.

When Edict of Invulnerability is effective, physical weapon damage is not a reliable kill plan. The main has one Bane of Spirit; clones have only their actual Bane Blade force dice/other owned damage sources, not five more rings. Conserve resources, use positioning and the real defensive durations, and test nonphysical damage against the actual protection. A swift dispel and the ring's swift activation cannot occur on the same unit in one turn.

### Against INT Sword Saint / Trickster

Trick Fate improves **that character's** rolls, not yours. It does not cancel your damage protection or guarantee that its attack's damage type penetrates it. Conversely, its legally owned force conversion or Swarmbane equipment can change the matchup. Last Stand, mutual critical immunity/weapon protection, concealment and ordinary damage determine whether a successful attack actually ends the fight.

Use Outflank only once actual allied engagement satisfies the rules, and Ever Ready only on real provocations. You do not retain Trick Fate yourself. Retained critical feats improve threats against a susceptible target; they do not make every20 lethal against Fiery Body. Six attack routines do not all resolve before the opponent's independently determined initiative unless the initiative order actually says so.
